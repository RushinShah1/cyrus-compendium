Choose the turn

reach? Is Cascade active? Check remaining Focus Points and the initiative order. Reassess after each
action if the target falls, moves, or forces a different plan.

Level 7 Trip and teleport
  Improvise Strategy [1]                 Trip [1]                                  Dimensional Assault [1]


1. Choose clumsy 1 on success. Improvise Strategy checks Warfare Lore against Perception DC within
30 feet. Choose a cue the target can perceive. Clumsy lowers Reflex DC for Trip and gives AC a status
penalty. Using Strategy leaves Cyrus’s first attack available. 2. Trip at full Athletics. Use the weapon’s
Trip trait for two-handed use at its reach. A success makes the target prone and off-guard. Clumsy’s AC
penalty can combine with off-guard’s circumstance penalty. A critical failure can put Cyrus prone, or a Trip
weapon can be dropped to take an ordinary failure. 3. Teleport if the destination buys something.
Assault costs a Focus Point, recharges Spellstrike and normally includes a melee Strike at second-attack
MAP, usually −5. Move to a clean flank, a safer lane or a square threatening a second foe. After
Dimensional Disappearance at 10, Cyrus can keep the invisibility and skip the Strike.

Choose Improvise Strategy → Trip → Recharge or Step if the teleport lacks value. If Strategy fails, judge the
unassisted Trip or redirect the turn. If Strategy leaves Cyrus off-guard on a critical failure, use positioning
or defence to answer it. Dimensional Assault · Trip trait

Positioning and later mobility
• Operate from reach. Use the outer ring for flanks, threat coverage and space from shorter-reach
  enemies. Ten-foot reach includes two squares diagonally. Flanking needs an opposite-edge or
  opposite-corner line between allies’ space centres and both allies able to attack the foe in melee. It makes
  the foe off-guard to their melee attacks. Reach · Flanking
• Set up the right ally. Stand opposite the Champion while staying in range of their reaction where
  possible. Check whether the second melee threat benefits more from a flank, or whether the casters need
  the target separated from friendlies for area effects. Give the party the landing square before pushing an
  enemy.
• Pick the area before committing. With Expansive Spellstrike, a burst centres on a corner of any square
  in the target’s space. A cone or line includes the target. It starts from Cyrus if adjacent, or any square
  adjacent to the target if he’s attacking from farther out. The area can catch allies. Compare the template
  with direct casting when range, cover, origin or safety changes the result. Expansive Spellstrike · Areas
• Plan the teleport around the goal. Dimensional Assault is half Speed until it heightens to 5th rank at
  level 9, then full Speed. Land somewhere visible that satisfies the spell’s enemy-reach wording and allows
  the included melee Strike. Use the hop where a normal route would take more actions or expose Cyrus to
  avoidable attacks. Dimensional Assault
• Spend movement and recharge together when available. Running Recharge combines recharge
  with a half-Speed Stride or a Step. Choose it over normal Recharge when repositioning changes a flank,
  ends pursuit, or makes a safe cast possible. Fused Staff and Running Recharge compete for the proposed
  level-8 class feat. Running Recharge

Example. Cyrus starts at one end of a straight corridor. Two foes are in the lane 20 and 40 feet away.
Start a 120-foot Lightning Bolt Sprinting Spellstrike, travel 45 feet through them, finish in a vacant square
5 feet beyond the second foe, and Strike that foe. With Expansive, the movement traces the line area. The
movement can pass through foes and avoids movement reactions. The activity still requires a legal Strike
after the move and a legal spell area. Save the last action for recharging, defence, or moving away once
the cast is resolved.

Keep priority targets within the lane Cyrus actually travels. The feat caps movement at the spell’s line
length. Its protection from reactions covers the movement. Check the spell’s Manipulate trait separately.
Use the table’s area convention for the line width and any effect beyond the landing square, especially
when Cyrus is Large. Sprinting Spellstrike · Line areas

Shove and Reposition are forced movement. Save Reactive Strike for their own move or another trigger. A
foe can provoke by standing after Trip. Standing in place resolves before the reaction. Movement and
reactions · Reactive Strike

Two-handed retrieval. Release one hand for free, draw a worn item with Interact, use it, then spend an
Interact to regrip. That is three actions for a normal one-action potion, four for a normal two-action scroll
with regrip. A preloaded Retrieval Prism or Belt can save the draw. A Potion Patch gives one-action self-use
for a suitable potion while holding the weapon. Leaving the grip loose can cost the Aspera Strike or
reaction you were holding for the next enemy. Interact · Retrieval Prism · Retrieval Belt · Potion Patch

Rules at a glance
 Rule                                   What to track

 Spellstrike · 2 actions                Cast a 1- or 2-action spell requiring attack roll or save and make a
                                        melee Strike. Attack spells use the Strike outcome. Save spells roll their
                                        own save, including on a normal miss. A critical Strike failure disrupts
                                        the spell. The weapon sets reach, and the activity normally affects just
                                        the Strike target. Both attack increments apply after the activity.
                                        Recharge with 1 concentrate action, Analysis, or a 1-action-or-longer
                                        conflux spell.

 Arcane Cascade · 1 action              Impossible Magic lets Cyrus enter directly. Melee Strikes gain arcane
                                        and +1 force damage, and Cyrus gets resistance 1 to spell damage.
                                        Both rise to 2 with Weapon Specialization and 3 with Greater Weapon
                                        Specialization. A spell cast earlier that turn can set the damage type. A
                                        studious spell permits free Cascade as the next action on that turn.

 Laughing Shadow · in Cascade           +5-foot status bonus to Speeds in armour or +10 unarmoured. The
                                        damage against off-guard foes rises to 3, 5 or 7 at the same
                                        specialization breakpoints with a completely free other hand. Use base
                                        damage with Aspera in both hands until its specific benefit is known.

 Magus’s Analysis · 1 action            Attempt creature Recall Knowledge, then recharge even if the check
                                        fails. A Strike hit against it earlier this turn gives +1 circumstance to the
                                        check. The subject is immune to this ability for 1 day. Use ordinary
                                        Recharge when needed.

 Force Fang · 1 action, 1 FP            Deals 1d4+1 force within melee reach without check or save, adding
                                        1d4+1 per two ranks. Recharges Spellstrike without MAP. Its
                                        Manipulate trait matters in threatening reach.

 Dimensional Assault · 1 action, 1 FP   Teleport to a visible space in range within reach of an enemy and Strike
                                        that enemy. Recharges Spellstrike. Carrying a creature through disrupts
                                        it. Dimensional Disappearance adds invisibility on arrival and lets Cyrus
                                        omit the Strike. If he Strikes, the invisibility ends after the attack.

 Improvise Strategy · 1 action          Master Warfare Lore. Select auditory or visual and check against
                                        Perception DC at 30 feet. Success chooses clumsy 1, dazzled, off-guard
                                        or stupefied 1 until end of Cyrus’s next turn. A target action can end the
                                        effect earlier, Concentrate for auditory or Interact for visual. Target is
                                        immune for 1 hour except on critical success. Critical failure leaves
                                        Cyrus off-guard until his next turn starts.

 Bon Mot · 1 action                     Diplomacy vs Will DC within 30 feet. Success gives –2 status to
                                        Perception and Will saves for 1 minute, critical success –3. A one-action
                                        retort can end it. Critical failure turns the success penalty onto Cyrus.

 Reactive Strike · reaction             Planned at level 6. Melee Strike on a creature within reach for the
                                        action’s Manipulate, movement, or ranged attack trigger. It ignores
                                        MAP. A critical hit disrupts a triggering Manipulate action. Weigh that
                                        chance against holding a defensive reaction.

 Shield · 1 action                      +1 circumstance AC to start of next turn without a free hand. Grants
                                        Shield Block with Hardness 5, +5 per two ranks, and can block spell or
                                        magical damage. Blocking ends the spell and prevents casting Shield
                                        for 10 minutes. Competes with Reactive Strike and other reactions.

Knowledge and scenes
Ask a question with an action attached. Examples include which save to target before a cast, what
stops the creature from getting back up, or whether moving or manipulating will provoke a reaction.
Arcana covers appropriate dragons, beasts and constructs. Society fits common humanoids and
organisations. Warfare Lore addresses formations, signals and tactics as applicable. Academia Lore fits
relevant institutions and scholars. Share unusual creature identification with Religion or Nature specialists
when their skill is stronger. Recall Knowledge · Creature identification

Use Undead Lore if this campaign regularly tests it, or Engineering Lore if wards, ruins and structures are
the recurrent problem. Unified Theory broadens Arcana for magical-tradition checks. Creature
identification continues to use the skills relevant to that creature. Unified Theory

Outside the fight, Cyrus can identify the right officer at a Cormyrean watch post, use Academia Lore to
read an institution’s habits, or give the group’s best speaker a concrete opening. At an old ward, find
whom the builders meant to admit and how, then choose whether to dispel, bypass, or parley. Smudge, a
scouting spell or a utility scroll can turn one piece of information into a route.
