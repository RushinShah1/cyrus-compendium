Cyrus’s Spell Selections
An arcane spellbook and casting kit for a reach-weapon Laughing Shadow Magus. Prepare by
encounter role, then use Aspera, a wand or a scroll to cover the jobs that recur outside your
spell slots.


Daily preparation
At level 3, a balanced starting slate is Gouging Claw, Shield, Needle Darts, Electric Arc and
Frostbite. This is a sample loadout. Swap Needle Darts for Figment or Telekinetic Hand when exploration
matters, or let an Ignition spellheart cover fire. Use Gouging Claw for the routine Strike and spend slots on
a stronger rider, a weak save or a team effect.

Cast normally or deliver with Aspera
• Attack payload. A charged Spellstrike takes two actions and accepts a one- or two-action spell that
  requires an attack roll or save. The weapon Strike result drives an attack-roll spell. Reach extends touch
  payloads like Gouging Claw. Sure Strike and off-guard improve this lane.
• Save payload. The target uses its normal save after an ordinary miss. A critically failed Strike disrupts the
  spell. Save-based riders apply after the Strike’s damage. A saving-throw spell cast separately usually
  leaves your Strike at full accuracy.
• Area payload. Expansive Spellstrike restores bursts, cones and lines with the chosen Strike target inside.
  With a nonadjacent target, a cone or line uses a source square adjacent to the target. Emanations, walls
  and effects that select multiple distinct targets still need ordinary casting for full coverage. The Grid Atlas
  shows friendly-fire placement for standard areas.

• Line movement. Sprinting Spellstrike takes two actions. With Expansive, it lays the line along the straight
  movement route before the Strike. You can move up to the spell’s line length and use a matching

  targets inside the line.
• Action trade. Spellstrike uses the charge and counts as two attacks for MAP afterward. Conflux casts such
  as Dimensional Assault and Force Fang can recharge it. Choose direct casting when keeping the charge,
  getting the full area, separating enemies or casting at safe range matters more than the added Strike.

Handling. A scroll or wand must be held and its spell on your eligible list. The item casts at a fixed rank
with your applicable spell attack and DC. The scroll is consumed. The standard wand works once daily and
can be overcharged once that day at the risk of breaking or destruction. Compare a 60 gp rank-1 wand to
a 4 gp rank-1 scroll, and a 160 gp rank-2 wand to a 12 gp rank-2 scroll. An 8-hour rank-2 Tailwind pays for
itself if it replaces fourteen rank-2 scrolls and gives daily access while the purchase is in use.

Read the ratings
Five-point ratings mark each spell’s meaningful roles. An omitted role scores 0. 1 is narrow, 3 is strong in
its job and 5 defines the role in Cyrus’s kit. Preparation, scroll and wand are separate ratings for the value
of that casting source. A dash indicates that the source is inapplicable. Linked spell names open the full
rules. Grades measure usefulness for Cyrus in the spell’s best role. S is a defining acquisition, A earns
regular use, B is a clean specialist, and C is a campaign or encounter pick. The rules summaries use CS, S,
F and CF for degrees of success.

Gouging Claw
S · Damage ★★★★★

2 actions · AC · touch · 1 creature

Rules. On hit choose piercing or slashing for 2d6 and add 2 persistent bleed. Both double on a critical hit.
Each additional rank adds 1d6 initial and 1 persistent bleed. Archives of Nethys

For Cyrus. Baseline reach Spellstrike. Against creatures immune to bleed, use the best alternative
damage type or save.

2 actions · basic Reflex · 30 feet · 1 or 2 creatures

Rules. Each of one or two creatures takes 2d4 electricity with a basic Reflex save. Each additional rank

For Cyrus. Default direct damage when both targets are useful. Spellstrike delivers it to one because the
spell selects separate targets.

Shield
S · Buff / Heal ★★★★☆

1 action · until the start of your next turn

Rules. Gain +1 circumstance AC until your next turn and access to Shield Block with Hardness 5. That
block can reduce damage from spells or magical effects. Blocking ends Shield and prevents another cast
for 10 minutes. Hardness rises by 5 per two ranks. Archives of Nethys

For Cyrus. A spare-action defence with both hands occupied. When a reaction is already reserved, the AC
can still matter.

Needle Darts
A · Damage ★★★★☆ · Utility ★★☆☆☆

2 actions · AC · 60 feet · 1 creature

Rules. Hit deals 3d4 piercing with the properties of a piece of metal you possess. Critical hit doubles
damage and adds 1 persistent bleed. Each additional rank adds 1d4 and 1 critical bleed. The metal
returns. Archives of Nethys

For Cyrus. Carry cold iron and silver for known weaknesses. A clean 60-foot fallback when Aspera cannot
reach.

Frostbite
A · Damage ★★★☆☆ · Debuff ★★☆☆☆

2 actions · basic Fortitude · 60 feet · 1 creature

Rules. Basic Fortitude against 2d4 cold. CF also grants weakness 1 to bludgeoning until your next turn.
Each additional rank adds 1d4 damage and 1 weakness. Archives of Nethys

For Cyrus. Repeatable Fortitude coverage at 60 feet. On a CF, call out the weakness for the party’s
bludgeoning attacks.

Ignition
A · Damage ★★★★☆

2 actions · AC · 30 feet · 1 creature

Rules. An attack hits for 2d4 fire. In melee reach choose a melee spell attack for d6s instead. Crit doubles
initial damage and adds 1 persistent die. Each additional rank adds one die to both amounts. Archives of
Nethys

For Cyrus. Best as a fire option from a spellheart if daily cantrip slots are tight. The critical-only rider is
less dependable than Gouging Claw’s bleed.

For Cyrus. Prepare when Cyrus leads investigation or when the party lacks repeatable detection.
