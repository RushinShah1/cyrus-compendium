owns permanent spending priorities.


Hands, retrieval, and the off-turn
Drawing and Stowing Items governs the grip changes. Interact draws a stored or worn object, passes a
held object to a willing creature with a free hand, or adds a hand to Aspera. Removing a hand or dropping
an object uses Release. Keep emergency consumables in accessible worn storage and pull packed items
out before combat. Swap trades a held item for a worn one with one Interact. Drinking, activating and
regripping still have their own actions.

Aspera must be wielded to make a two-handed Strike or Reactive Strike with it. End a turn in the grip you
want during enemy movement. If an empty container stays in hand, drop it with Release rather than
spend time stowing it. Free-action retrieval without a trigger is a turn tool, so hold an item in advance if its
activation responds to an off-turn trigger. Draw and other manipulate actions can provoke a reaction from
a creature that has one. Worn manipulate activations, including Blast Boots, require a free hand to touch
the item. Activating Items

Bow and throwable backup. A bow listed with Hands 1+ can be carried in one hand. Firing needs the
other hand for the shot. At reload 0, drawing ammunition and firing are part of the Strike. A vial, scroll, or
Aspera still in that second hand stops the bow shot. An ammunition item's separate activation can add
actions. A light hammer in one hand leaves the other free, and its thrown attack is a ranged Strike, so it
uses Dexterity for the attack and normally adds Strength to damage. Compare its backup role with Needle
Darts in the Combat Flow guide. Player Core weapons · Magical ammunition

Affix a Talisman takes 10 minutes and a repair toolkit in both hands. Wear or wield the host item when
using its talisman. Two talismans on one host suppress both under the ordinary rule. Ghostbane and
Escape Fulu carry that trait. A prism or grasshopper therefore competes for the armor’s slot. A crystal or
Ghostbane Fulu competes for Aspera’s. Striker’s Scroll also occupies Aspera’s talisman slot, as explained
in Feat Paths and Spell Selections. Other fulu use Affix a Fulu and typically take effect once placed. Fulu
expire a year after creation unless specified otherwise.


Potions and elixirs
Potion and elixir entries below use one held hand and one manipulate action to drink. Treat the action cost
and remaining encounter length as part of the price. Feed a potion at normal reach to a willing or helpless
creature under the potion rules. Put the item on an ally with a free hand if they’re better placed to deliver
it.

 Type                                   Example and use

 Oils                                   Oil of Skating, item 2 and 5 gp, applies to feet or footwear with two
                                        hands and 1 manipulate action. For 1 hour, +10-foot status to Speed on
                                        flat ground or +20 downhill. Difficult, greater difficult, and uneven
                                        ground remove the bonus. Uphill becomes difficult terrain and Balance
                                        checks worsen by one degree. Oil of Potency uses two hands and 1
                                        manipulate action. Item 2, 7 gp, supplies +1 striking weapon or +1
                                        resilient armor for 1 minute. Oil of Mending, item 3 and 9 gp, applies
                                        with two hands and repairs an item as 2nd-rank Mending.

 Bottled breaths                        The bottled breath trait usually takes one hand to inhale or administer.
                                        Effects last while holding breath, normally up to 5 + Constitution
                                        modifier rounds before action or damage costs. Blight Breath, item 12,
                                        350 gp, takes 1 manipulate to inhale for poison resistance 10. Its
                                        separate 1-action exhale makes a 15-foot cone, 10d6 poison, DC 29
                                        basic Reflex, and expends the dose. Buy only for poison work where a
                                        cone is also useful.

 Teas                                   AoN tea index · Energizing Tea entry. One dose costs 20 gp. A one-action
                                        drink gives +1 item to Athletics and Acrobatics for 10 minutes. A
                                        10-minute ceremony with sterling tea ware extends it to 1 hour. Use a
                                        dose for a climb, contest or court demonstration when a permanent item
                                        bonus is smaller.

 Missives                               The level-5 Butterfly Heartening Missive costs 25 gp. Compose to a
                                        known recipient and location. It flies there at about 15 miles per hour for
                                        up to 24 hours. On activation, once in the next 24 hours it improves an
                                        unsuccessful Diplomacy or Performance check by one degree, a fortune
                                        effect. Bull, Rabbit, and Turtle instead grant +1 item to Fortitude, Reflex,
                                        or Will for 24 hours at item 6 and 50 gp.

 Explosive missive                      Explosive Missive uses a composed message and 2 actions with
                                        concentrate and manipulate. Pick acid, cold, electricity, fire, or sonic
                                        through its inscription. It deals 4d6 in a 5-foot burst from a corner of the
                                        missive’s space, DC 18 basic Reflex. Critical failure adds 1d4 persistent
                                        of the same type. Use as a prepared environmental tool and check
                                        friendlies at the blast point.

 Bombs                                  Lesser Bottled Lightning is item 1 and 3 gp. One-action ranged Strike
                                        deals 1d6 electricity and 1 splash. A hit makes the main target off-guard
                                        until the start of the thrower’s next turn. Let a reliable ranged ally use it
                                        to set Cyrus up when nobody can flank.

 Alchemical tools                       Lesser Smoke Ball is item 1 and 3 gp. Two hands, 1 manipulate action,
                                        5-foot burst from a corner of the user’s space. One minute of mutual
                                        concealment unless strong wind disperses it. Use for a withdrawal or
                                        crossing, and plan around allies’ targeting checks.

 Hostile fulu                           Stumbling Fulu is item 3 and 10 gp. Affix to armor. For 1 hour, after a
                                        completed Stride the wearer attempts DC 17 Reflex. Failure gives
                                        clumsy 1. Critical failure gives clumsy 2 and prone. A preplanned trick
                                        for someone whose armor you can reach, with a fixed DC.
