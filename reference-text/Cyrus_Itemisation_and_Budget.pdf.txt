Mundane loadout. Check the live character sheet first. Breastplate costs 8 gp, with AC +4 and Dex cap
+1. Hide is 2 gp, with AC +3 and Dex cap +2. Both reach the same baseline AC if the Dexterity cap is
filled. With the listed Strength threshold met, the ordinary 5-foot armour Speed penalty drops to zero.
Keep an adventurer’s pack, the spellbook, and a cheap light hammer if you want a carried bludgeoning
backup. Needle Darts with suitable metal covers most ranged weakness tests without buying a second
runed weapon. Armour rules

Automatic Bonus Progression branch. Use the standard rune cart unless the campaign confirms
Automatic Bonus Progression. Under that variant, inherent attack, damage dice, AC and saving-throw
advances arrive at the corresponding levels. Cross off those fundamental rune purchases and reassess
purchases for an item bonus alone, including Athletics and Arcana gear. The variant can change which
runes remain available. Keep the prices below as comparison points for useful activations, spell access
and remaining property options.

A 75 gp level-3 lump-sum kit can buy +1 potency, breastplate, an adventurer’s pack and Ancestral
Geometry for 74.5 gp if the weapon itself is supplied. The other starting-wealth route gives one level-2
permanent item, two level-1 permanent items and 25 gp instead. If the delivered Aspera already covers
potency, move its reserved 35 gp toward the first spellheart or gloves. A gift or loot parcel changes the
worksheet, so record which upgrades are already owned before spending.

For the bespoke stave, use the final item price once assigned. Published examples give comparison points.
Spellstriker Staff is 360 gp at 7 and 1,400 gp at 11. Staff of the Unblinking Eye is 230 gp at 6 and 900 gp at
10. Avoid funding a published stopgap and a replacement in the same shopping band without a specific
reason.

• Ancestral Geometry. At preparations, keep the higher of two d20 rolls and randomly determine the
  covered save. Its once-daily fortune reaction replaces the appropriate save roll with the stored result. The
  tattoo also grants an ancestor-related Lore item bonus.
• Weapon spellheart. Clay Sphere grants Gouging Claw. Activating the sphere to cast a morph or
  polymorph spell gives all three physical damage choices for 1 minute and a larger weapon damage die
  through the end of the next turn. Flaming Star grants Ignition and adds 1d4 fire to weapon Strikes through
  the next turn after its fire activation. Jolt Coil grants Electric Arc and has a parallel electricity rider, or
  provides passive electricity resistance on armour.
• Long-term spellheart. Greater Phantasmal Doorknob gives Figment and daily rank-4 Vision of Death.
  Weapon critical hits blind through the target’s next turn, followed by immunity to that blindness for 24
  hours. The mental and emotion traits matter against immune targets. This rider uses no setup action.
• Healing. The gloves heal an adjacent, willing living creature for 2d6+7 as one action once per day and
  give +1 item to Medicine. Greater at 9 costs 700 gp and upgrades these to 4d6+15 and +2. The unique
  Kindler Cameo is a separate level-7, 300 gp self-recovery option. Its two-action daily activation restores
  3d10+12, clears current persistent damage and grants +2 status to Will for 1 minute.
• Athletics. Lifting Belt gives +1 item to Athletics and increases the carrying thresholds by 1 Bulk.
  Armbands of Athleticism at level 9 for 645 gp give +2 item to Athletics and +5 feet on successful Climb
  and Swim checks. Item bonuses overlap with the weapon’s applicable Trip item bonus. Revisit the
  armbands if Athletics is a broad job or the +2 comes online before +2 weapon potency.
• Actions. Retrieval Belt stores a held item of up to 1 Bulk with one action, then retrieves it as a free action
  into a free hand. It locks out further use for 1 minute after retrieval. Greater (9, 600 gp) stores three and
  major (13, 2,500 gp) ten. Tactician’s Helm gives +1 item to Warfare Lore and banks up to two charges
  from Reactive Strike hits. Its once-per-hour, one-action activation spends a charge to double Stride, boost
  Athletics and Acrobatics for the turn, reposition with a shield or Interact up to three times.
• Mobility and initiative. Boots of Bounding give +5-foot item Speed, longer Leaps and +2 to jumps.
  Propulsive Boots are a level-13, 3,000 gp alternative with +5-foot item Speed (land, climb, and swim) and
  a one-action daily activation. It provides 1 minute of quickened for Stride, Climb, or Swim. Mobile is the
  level-5, 160 gp armour rune that extends one Step each turn by 5 feet. Sunweave is a level-7, 350 gp
  armour rune with +1 item Perception and a once-daily initiative fortune roll. At one slot, buy Mobile for
  recurring tactical spacing or Sunweave for the Perception and first-round setup. Status Speed bonuses
  such as Tailwind and Arcane Cascade use the better value.
• Magus kit. Master Magus Ring grants +2 item Arcana, a once-daily free-action focus point for a conflux
  spell that must be spent that turn, and a separate once-daily free-action teleport to a visible empty space
  within Speed. Buy for those activations if another item supplies the same Arcana bonus.

An affixed item can hold one spellheart or one talisman. Separate weapon and armour attachments give
separate slots. Spellheart cantrips heighten and can use Cyrus’s better statistics. Ranked spellheart casts
use the printed statistics and item-specific frequency. Spellheart trait

At preparation, count armour and other invested gear against the daily cap of 10. Removing an item
leaves its old investment on that day’s tally. Keep an accessible rescue item and one scheduled-use
consumable ready. Scroll and wand spells use their own casting actions and need the item held. The spell
and consumable references cover the actual loadout. Invest an Item · Wands
