Magus rules at the table
 Ability                              Reference and decision

 Spellstrike                          2 actions, charged. Combine a melee Strike with an eligible spell that takes
                                      1 or 2 actions. A spell attack uses the Strike result. A save spell still prompts
                                      the target’s save on an ordinary miss, and a critical failure on the Strike
                                      disrupts the spell. Spellstrike counts as two attacks for MAP, which applies
                                      after the activity. Recharge for 1 action with concentrate, or cast a conflux
                                      spell of at least 1 action.

 Arcane Cascade and Laughing Shadow   1 action, concentrate, stance. Melee Strikes deal 1 extra force damage
                                      and gain the arcane trait. Gain resistance 1 to damage from spells. Both
                                      values rise to 2 with weapon specialization and 3 with greater weapon
                                      specialization. After casting a spell earlier on the same turn, he can choose
                                      one damage type the spell can deal. Laughing Shadow adds +5 feet status to
                                      Speeds, or +10 feet unarmored. Its free-hand melee Strikes against off-guard
                                      creatures raise Cascade damage to 3/5/7. Cyrus’s Aspera discussion covers
                                      how the two-handed weapon can support that benefit.

 Dimensional Assault                  1 action, 1 Focus Point, concentrate. Teleport to a visible square within
                                      half Speed and make a melee Strike against a foe within reach from there.
                                      The range becomes full Speed at rank 5. Taking another creature disrupts the
                                      spell. The conflux cast recharges Spellstrike, while the Strike increases MAP.

 Magus’s Analysis                     1 action. Recall Knowledge about a creature, then recharge even if the
                                      check fails. A previous hit on that creature this turn grants +1 circumstance
                                      to the check. Each subject is immune for 1 day. Choose it when learning a
                                      defense or reaction matters.

 Force Fang                           1 action, 1 Focus Point, manipulate. A target within melee reach takes
                                      1d4+1 force, automatic, plus 1d4+1 per 2 heightened ranks. The cast
                                      recharges Spellstrike without adding MAP. Aspera’s reach extends the target
                                      options.

 Expansive Spellstrike                Bursts, cones, and lines take their normal area and must include the Strike
                                      target. The target’s occupied corner anchors a burst. For a cone or line from
                                      nonadjacent reach, pick any square adjacent to the target as the origin. All
                                      creatures in the area resolve the spell normally. Only one creature receives
                                      the weapon Strike.

 Reactive Strike                      Reaction, within reach. Triggered by a manipulate or move action, ranged
                                      attack, or leaving a square during movement. Make a melee Strike without
                                      applying or increasing MAP. A critical hit disrupts a triggering manipulate
                                      action. Trip can prompt a Stand and create an opening in Cyrus’s reach.

 Running Recharge                     1 action, concentrate. Recharge and Step or Stride up to half Speed. Take
                                      it early if movement and recharging repeatedly compete for the third action.

 Fused Staff                          Fuse a magical staff during daily preparation or with 10 minutes and
                                      manipulate. The fused item retains staff activations and casts after
                                      preparation. Use the higher of each fundamental weapon rune and suppress
                                      staff property runes. The fusion works for Cyrus and lasts until a new fusion or
                                      24 hours out of his possession. It earns a slot when the staff’s actual daily
                                      castings matter.

 Dimensional Disappearance            Dimensional Assault provides invisibility after teleporting. Cyrus can skip the
                                      Strike to stay invisible and leave MAP clear. If he Strikes, invisibility ends after
                                      that Strike, so it can benefit that attack.

 Ability                             Reference and decision

 Sprinting Spellstrike               2 actions, charged, line spell. Move in a straight line before the Strike, up
                                     to the spell’s line length. Movement can pass through enemies and provokes
                                     no reactions. With the movement Speed, can Burrow, Climb, Fly, or Swim
                                     instead of Striding. Expansive Spellstrike makes the spell area follow the

 Hasted Assault                      1 action, 1 Focus Point, manipulate, 1 minute. Recharges and grants
                                     quickened for Stride or Strike from the next turn. Best when Cyrus can start
                                     before the fight or early enough to gain more than a single extra action. The
                                     quickened action can pay for the named basic actions only.

 Arcane Shroud                       Select three spells on taking the feat. Each time Cyrus uses Arcane Cascade,
                                     one selected spell affects him until the end of his next turn or earlier if the
                                     stance or spell ends. Good first trio is Invisibility, Resist Energy, and Fleet
                                     Step for three distinct problems. The complete choice list is those three plus
                                     False Vitality, Mountain Resilience, and See the Unseen.

 Spell Retaliation                   Free action, uncharged. Recharge after succeeding at a save against an
                                     enemy spell or an enemy failing a spell attack against Cyrus. Useful in a fight
                                     with frequent hostile casts. Reactive Strike has wider application at 6.

 Knowledge Is Power                  A critical Recall Knowledge success lets Cyrus exploit a −1 circumstance
                                     penalty on the target’s defenses against his next relevant attack or spell. The
                                     target also takes the penalty on its next relevant attack or DC against him.
                                     Allies can benefit if he shares the information. The opportunity lasts one
                                     minute.

 Capture Magic                       Reaction. A successful save against a hostile spell or the enemy’s missed
                                     spell attack lets Cyrus enter Cascade using that spell to set the damage type.
                                     If already in Cascade, he can instead gain +2 status to its extra damage and
                                     resistance until the end of his next turn. Consider it in caster-heavy fights
                                     where his reaction is often free.

 Dispelling Spellstrike              3 actions. On a hit, also attempt to counteract one active spell on the target.
                                     Counteract rank is half level rounded up and the modifier is spellcasting
                                     proficiency plus Intelligence and applicable bonuses. Worth the full turn when
                                     a common buff or ward is materially protecting the enemy.


Studious Spell. Add the listed spells to the spellbook if needed. Casting one lets Cyrus enter Arcane
Cascade as a free action immediately afterward on the same turn. It adds spellbook access and action
economy. Normal spell slots supply the preparations. Remastered study rules

                                           legendary at 15 through its own increases. Each repeat names a

 Canny Acumen and Fleet                    Canny Acumen grants expert Perception in this build and master at
                                           17. Magus already reaches expert Perception at 9, so the early lead
                                           runs through 8. Fleet increases Speed by 5 feet and supports Stride,
                                           partial-Speed effects, and Dimensional Assault.

 Toughness and Incredible Initiative       Toughness adds maximum HP equal to level and lowers recovery
                                           check DC to 9 + dying value. Incredible Initiative gives +2
                                           circumstance to initiative. An early turn establishes a flank, trips a
                                           foe before melee allies act, or delivers party control.

                                           option that grants a general feat of level 7 or lower. Meet the chosen
                                           feat’s prerequisites.

 Cognitive Crossover                       The legacy feat lists Pathfinder Society membership as access.
                                           Choose two eligible knowledge skills when taking it. On a check with
                                           one that yields no information, use a reaction to try again with the
                                           other. Choose a pair whose actual subjects overlap. Arcana and
                                           Society suit parts of Cyrus’s campaign, while one named Lore can
                                           help if it addresses frequent questions.

 Magical Shorthand                         On a successful Learn a Spell check, improve to critical success and
                                           finish in 10 minutes. Failure allows another try after a week or level
                                           gain, whichever comes first. In downtime, use the applicable magical
                                           skill to earn a discount rather than income. Expert Arcana at 5
                                           satisfies the prerequisite for the level-6 slot.

 Improvise Strategy                        1 action, master Warfare Lore. Choose auditory or visual and test
                                           Warfare Lore against a creature’s Perception DC within 30 feet.
                                           Success applies clumsy 1, dazzled, off-guard, or stupefied 1 until the

                                           using concentrate for auditory or Interact for visual. A critical success
                                           avoids the one-hour reuse immunity. A failure still applies the
                                           immunity. A critical failure also makes Cyrus off-guard until the start
                                           of his next turn. It has mental and bravado traits and leaves the first
                                           attack clear.

 Unmistakable Lore                         Expert in a Lore qualifies. A trained Lore turns a Recall Knowledge

                                           critical success. The same protection covers all Cyrus’s trained Lores.

according to the encounters Cyrus is actually facing.
