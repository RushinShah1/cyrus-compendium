Aspera
A two-handed signature blade for Cyrus’s Laughing Shadow. The weapon’s reach sets the
rhythm, Time and Mind guide its growth, and the stave gives him practical spells he can reach
for without changing grips.


The weapon today
Alex approved the signature weapon and gave Aspera to Cyrus at level 3. He was receptive to the
two-handed Laughing Shadow interaction. Cyrus has the weapon. Its Foundry entry and in-character
identification are pending. The entries below are the build plan.

 Decision                           Working design

 Shape                              A curved, double-bladed scimitar on a long haft. Mechanically a two-handed
                                    martial polearm, with 1d8 slashing, Bulk 2, and Reach as the planning chassis.

 Handling                           Trip is the key proposed trait. It gives Cyrus a reason to alternate setup and
                                    weapon attacks while holding Aspera. Deadly d10 is the proposed critical flourish.
                                    The fauchard, glaive, and guisarme are useful published comparisons.

 Two-handed Arcane Cascade          While Cyrus wields Aspera in two hands, let qualifying Strikes against off-guard
                                    creatures use Laughing Shadow’s enhanced Arcane Cascade damage as if he had
                                    the free hand it normally requires. The bonus is 3, then 5 with weapon
                                    specialization, then 7 with greater specialization.

 Fighting style                     Reach keeps a flank open, gives more origin choices for Expansive Spellstrike,
                                    and covers approach lanes for Reactive Strike. Trip gives allies an opening if a
                                    spell slot would be too expensive.


Reach gives a Medium wielder 10-foot reach with a reach weapon. The 10-foot exception covers two
diagonal squares. Trip uses the weapon’s reach and potency item bonus and waives the free-hand
requirement for the Athletics action. Trip’s action tests Reflex DC and leaves the target prone on success.
End a turn with both hands on Aspera if you want its reaction threat. Restoring the grip costs an Interact.


Relic growth
Time and Mind are the proposed mechanical aspects. Luck can show in divergent outcomes, and shadow
in the blade’s presentation. The GM Core relic schedule places minor gifts at 1 and 5, major gifts at 9 and
13, and a grand gift at 17. Aspera’s first manifestation can happen later than its arrival. Gift saves use the
better of Cyrus’s class DC and spell DC.

A grand gift can follow the campaign’s late story. Time Reversal is the time-focused candidate. Mental
Bastion is the knowledge-focused comparison. Another level-17 direction is an Intelligence apex
awakening, optionally with a narrow Lore item bonus. Pick an awakening that reflects the story Aspera has
developed by then.


The fused stave
Use the remastered Fused Staff feat at level 8 if the stave is ready. Fuse the magical staff into Aspera
during daily preparation or with a 10-minute manipulate activity. The fusion keeps the staff’s activations,
including normal casting from a prepared staff while wielding the weapon. The pair uses the higher
potency and striking runes independently. Staff property runes are suppressed in fusion. It lasts until
Cyrus fuses the staff again or loses possession of the weapon for 24 hours, and its fused benefits work for
him alone.

Under the staff rules, prepare one staff per day. It starts with charges equal to the rank of the highest spell
slot. As a prepared caster Cyrus can expend one slot in daily preparations to add its rank in charges. Each
ranked cast uses charges equal to the printed rank of that entry and uses Cyrus’s casting statistics. Staff
cantrips cost no charges and heighten normally. The spell must be on an available spell list and within
Cyrus’s casting access. The stave adds casts to the lower ranks Cyrus retains.

Spell lists by milestone
The proposed multipurpose item draws on time, cognition, and illusions. The published personal staff
framework uses one shared trait and its own item-level table, with ranks 3 through 6 first appearing at
item levels 9, 11, 13, and 15. Aspera’s 8/10/12/14 steps and theme-based spell selection are bespoke. The
staff-count audit found means of 6.69, 10.00, and 11.36 listed spells at item levels 8, 10, and 12
respectively. Aspera’s core steps give 7, 9, and 11, with lower-rank alternates competing within entries.

At level 16, a rank-8 slot gives 8 charges at preparation. That buys Haste plus Synesthesia, or True
Target plus Sure Strike. Sacrificing one rank-7 preparation brings the pool to 15, enough for Haste, True
Target and Synesthesia across the day if Cyrus has the casting access for each. Use the normal rank-7
preparations for Spell Riposte, Defy the Gods, Frigid Flurry or Comet Charge when an encounter calls for
them. Spell Riposte can instead take the stave’s True Target entry for a more reactive build.
