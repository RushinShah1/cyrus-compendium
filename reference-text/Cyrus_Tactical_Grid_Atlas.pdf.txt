Before committing the spell
A burst’s selected corner does not need its own weapon reach check. The Strike reaches the creature,
then the feat picks the anchor. With a cone, start at Cyrus when he is adjacent. Otherwise choose a
square adjacent to the target as the source, then aim so the target is included.

The spell’s area can resolve on an ordinary Strike miss. A disrupted spell, such as on a critical Strike failure,
produces no burst or cone.
