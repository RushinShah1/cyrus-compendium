# Arcane spell value for a remastered Laughing Shadow Magus (Cyrus): cantrips and ranks 1-9 as Spellstrike payloads, direct casts, scrolls, wands and staves

Scope notes for the report writer:
- The remastered Magus is printed in *Impossible Magic* (Paizo, 2026). Archives of Nethys (AoN) now hosts remaster pages for Spellstrike (Impossible Magic pg. 11), Expansive Spellstrike (pg. 17), Laughing Shadow (pg. 13) and Sprinting Spellstrike (pg. 24). The old AoN class page (Classes.aspx?ID=67) is the *legacy* Secrets of Magic version.
- The main expert source is the community guide **"Martial Magicks"** by Nintendogeek01 (Google Doc, last updated 09/13/2026, remaster-updated for Impossible Magic). It uses a 1-4 star scale (4 = best). Below, "MM" means this guide. The guide is about 565k characters long, so I read it in excerpts through a summarising fetch tool, and some spell entries fell between excerpts (noted under Gaps).
- RPGBOT's "remastered arcane spell list" is a general arcane-caster view, not a magus one. It is marked incomplete and I only got its cantrip to rank 2 section.
- I found no usable YouTube transcripts (How It's Played, Rules Lawyer, Nonat1s) and no recent r/Pathfinder2e threads specific to the Impossible Magic magus within the tool budget. Treat the "community consensus" below as mostly MM plus RPGBOT, not a broad poll.

**Two corrections to the brief:**
1. On the remaster AoN page, **Expansive Spellstrike is a level 2 feat**, not level 4 — [AoN Expansive Spellstrike](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?ID=9047).
2. **Laughing Shadow's extra off-guard damage needs a completely free hand.** "The other hand must be completely free. A free-hand weapon or other item in that hand prevents the extra damage" — [AoN Laughing Shadow (remaster)](https://2e.aonprd.com/HybridStudies.aspx?ID=11). A two-handed reach weapon user like Cyrus will usually lose this bonus, but keeps the Speed bonus. That makes speed-buff spells (Tailwind, Fleet Step) worth less to him and damage payloads worth more.

## Q1. Best cantrips for remaster Spellstrike, and how remaster Spellstrike handles save and multi-target spells

### Takeaway
Under the remaster rules, Spellstrike takes any 1- or 2-action spell with a spell attack or a save. The Strike's roll decides attack spells. Save spells still force their save on a miss, and only a critical failure on the Strike stops them. Single-target attack cantrips lead the consensus: Gouging Claw (MM: "MADE for the Magus"), then Ignition and Needle Darts, with Live Wire close behind. Electric Arc has fallen to 2 stars in MM. Frostbite and Caustic Blast are low-rated save cantrips, though Frostbite is useful because it still lands when the Strike misses.

### Cited Findings
**Rules (remaster, Impossible Magic):**
- Spellstrike (2 actions, frequency "until recharged"): you cast a spell that takes 1 or 2 actions and needs a spell attack roll or a saving throw, and channel it into a melee Strike. The Strike gains the arcane trait — [AoN Spellstrike](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=4260)
- The Strike counts as two attacks for the multiple attack penalty (MAP), applied after the Spellstrike resolves — [AoN Spellstrike](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=4260)
- Spell attack spells use the Strike's result for both the Strike and the spell — [AoN Spellstrike](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=4260)
- Save spells: the target saves normally whatever your attack roll was, unless the Strike critically fails, which disrupts the spell — [AoN Spellstrike](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=4260)
- Strike and spell damage are combined when applying resistances and weaknesses — [AoN Spellstrike](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=4260)
- Recharge: a 1-action concentrate activity, or casting a conflux spell of at least 1 action. Other focus spells don't recharge it — [AoN Spellstrike](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=4260)
- MM on targeting: "One Target: Only the creature you hit with your weapon will be affected by the spell," so multi-target spells lose value. If the target is immune to the spell, the spell is wasted even if the Strike hits. You can't use the 3-action version of a variable-action spell with Spellstrike — [Martial Magicks](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/d/1E_MwD2ewUIRcb29tr0KXUBfCmXbBNe_2im4-5Q6fRmI/mobilebasic)
- MM: save spells still matter for Spellstrike because they take effect on a miss that isn't a critical miss. The magus's lower spell attack and DC make many spells that are good on full casters only middling when cast outright — [Martial Magicks](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/d/1E_MwD2ewUIRcb29tr0KXUBfCmXbBNe_2im4-5Q6fRmI/mobilebasic)
- Cantrips auto-heighten to half level rounded up. MM rates cantrips as a class feature at 4 stars — [Martial Magicks](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/d/1E_MwD2ewUIRcb29tr0KXUBfCmXbBNe_2im4-5Q6fRmI/mobilebasic)

**Cantrip ratings (MM, 1-4 stars):**
- **Gouging Claw (4 stars):** "This spell was MADE for the Magus" — [Martial Magicks](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/d/1E_MwD2ewUIRcb29tr0KXUBfCmXbBNe_2im4-5Q6fRmI/mobilebasic). RPGBOT calls it "the melee attack cantrip benchmark" with good damage plus persistent damage on a hit — [RPGBOT arcane list](https://rpgbot.net/p2/characters/remastered-arcane-spell-list)
- **Ignition (4 stars):** fire damage, ranked just behind Gouging Claw, with dice that become d6s when cast in melee — [Martial Magicks](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/d/1E_MwD2ewUIRcb29tr0KXUBfCmXbBNe_2im4-5Q6fRmI/mobilebasic). RPGBOT: good single-target damage and persistent damage on a crit — [RPGBOT](https://rpgbot.net/p2/characters/remastered-arcane-spell-list)
- **Needle Darts (4 stars):** 3d4 up-front damage, 60 ft range, and can exploit metal weaknesses. Falls behind Gouging Claw and Ignition as ranks rise — [Martial Magicks](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/d/1E_MwD2ewUIRcb29tr0KXUBfCmXbBNe_2im4-5Q6fRmI/mobilebasic). RPGBOT notes Pathfinder Society requires holding a piece of the metal to use the weakness feature — [RPGBOT](https://rpgbot.net/p2/characters/remastered-arcane-spell-list)
- **Live Wire (4 stars):** electricity damage that still deals some damage on a miss — [Martial Magicks](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/d/1E_MwD2ewUIRcb29tr0KXUBfCmXbBNe_2im4-5Q6fRmI/mobilebasic). RPGBOT disagrees: "Ignition does the same job, scales better, and works in melee" — [RPGBOT](https://rpgbot.net/p2/characters/remastered-arcane-spell-list)
- **Electric Arc (2 stars):** "Oh how the mighty have fallen." MM gives its damage as "(1d4*rank)+1d4" and says it becomes "a much more solid pick" only for Twofold Tine with Split Spellstrike — [Martial Magicks](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/d/1E_MwD2ewUIRcb29tr0KXUBfCmXbBNe_2im4-5Q6fRmI/mobilebasic). In contrast, RPGBOT (for blaster casters) still calls it "the gold standard of ranged attack cantrips" — [RPGBOT](https://rpgbot.net/p2/characters/remastered-arcane-spell-list)
- **Frostbite (2 stars):** a save-based cantrip, so it can still force a save after a missed Strike, but its damage is unremarkable — [Martial Magicks](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/d/1E_MwD2ewUIRcb29tr0KXUBfCmXbBNe_2im4-5Q6fRmI/mobilebasic)
- **Caustic Blast (1 star):** depends on Reflex saves against the magus's lower DC, with "somewhat low damage" — [Martial Magicks](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/d/1E_MwD2ewUIRcb29tr0KXUBfCmXbBNe_2im4-5Q6fRmI/mobilebasic). RPGBOT: only better than Electric Arc against 3+ targets or for persistent damage on a critical failure — [RPGBOT](https://rpgbot.net/p2/characters/remastered-arcane-spell-list)
- **Ray of Frost (3 stars):** 120 ft range, modest damage. **Telekinetic Projectile (3 stars):** choice of physical damage type, 30 ft range. **Tangle Vine (2 stars):** no damage, but a speed penalty or immobilise; good for capture-focused days — [Martial Magicks](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/d/1E_MwD2ewUIRcb29tr0KXUBfCmXbBNe_2im4-5Q6fRmI/mobilebasic)
- **Detect Magic (4 stars)** as a utility cantrip — [Martial Magicks](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/d/1E_MwD2ewUIRcb29tr0KXUBfCmXbBNe_2im4-5Q6fRmI/mobilebasic)
- **Thunderstrike** is a rank 1 slot spell, not a cantrip (see Q2).

### Inferences
- For Cyrus (Strength-based, high weapon attack, Int secondary), attack-roll cantrips (Gouging Claw, Ignition, Needle Darts) use his weapon attack roll, so his secondary Int doesn't weaken them. Save cantrips use his lower class DC and become a "miss insurance" option. That makes **Frostbite** (cold, Fortitude) a sensible way to target Fortitude and to cover cold weakness or electricity/fire resistance, despite its 2-star rating.
- Defence coverage from cantrips: AC (Gouging Claw slashing/piercing, Ignition fire, Needle Darts piercing + metal, Live Wire electricity), Fortitude (Frostbite), Reflex (Caustic Blast). No top-tier cantrip targets Will.
- Gouging Claw's persistent bleed and Ignition's fire give two damage types to work around resistances.

### Gaps
- I didn't find MM's full reasoning for Gouging Claw (the excerpt was cut off), or MM ratings for Void Warp, Scatter Scree, Slashing Gust or Shield.
- The Spellstrike rules page I fetched doesn't address multi-target, area or reach directly. MM and Expansive Spellstrike fill this in.
- The March 2024 "Magus errata" r/Pathfinder2e thread appeared in search but I didn't read it. Its findings predate Impossible Magic.

## Q2. Top arcane picks per rank 1-9 for spellbook/slots, and which are better as scrolls or wands

### Takeaway
MM's consensus favours spells that **don't depend on the magus's middling spell DC**: Sure Strike, Haste, Invisibility (rank 4), Fly, Wall of Stone, Disintegrate (with Sure Strike), True Target, Disappearance and Foresight. Glacial Skewer, Weight of the World, Acid Grip, Thunderstrike and Cave Fangs are the best-rated payload or control spells. Spells that rely on a failed save, such as Blindness, Paralyze, Petrify, Phantasmagoria and Chain Lightning, are usually rated 2 stars, and sustained spells (Laughing Fit, Quandary) rate 1 star. Long-duration, everyday buffs (Tailwind, Invisibility, Haste) are the consensus wand picks, and "just in case" utility belongs on scrolls.

### Cited Findings (all from [Martial Magicks](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/d/1E_MwD2ewUIRcb29tr0KXUBfCmXbBNe_2im4-5Q6fRmI/mobilebasic) unless another source is named)

**Rank 1**
- **Sure Strike (4 stars):** negates concealed and hidden, ignores circumstance penalties, and lets you roll twice. MM calls it a key reason to get a Ring of Wizardry. RPGBOT: weak early, strong later, and casting it first makes attack spells like Disintegrate more reliable — [RPGBOT](https://rpgbot.net/p2/characters/remastered-arcane-spell-list)
- **Thunderstrike (3 stars):** penalises saves for metal-using targets, can make them clumsy, and pairs well with Spellstrike. RPGBOT: about 9 average damage per rank on a basic Reflex save; "Not very exciting, but decently effective and very simple" — [RPGBOT](https://rpgbot.net/p2/characters/remastered-arcane-spell-list)
- **Fleet Step (3 stars):** a large speed boost for one minute, and an Arcane Shroud option at level 14. RPGBOT: situational, and Tailwind is usually better — [RPGBOT](https://rpgbot.net/p2/characters/remastered-arcane-spell-list)
- **Grease (3 stars):** difficult terrain to slow enemies or help allies escape. RPGBOT: a fair action trade on two enemies and "a great trade" on three — [RPGBOT](https://rpgbot.net/p2/characters/remastered-arcane-spell-list)
- **Tailwind (3 stars):** MM says Laughing Shadows already have a speed bonus, so the rank 2 version is recommended as a wand for other magi.
- **Fear (2 stars):** frightens on a success, and improves at rank 3 by hitting five targets. RPGBOT: "Isn't bad," but Demoralize plus another spell usually does more — [RPGBOT](https://rpgbot.net/p2/characters/remastered-arcane-spell-list)
- **Breathe Fire (2 stars):** overshadowed by other blasts by rank 3. RPGBOT rates it poor: cantrip-level damage at much shorter range — [RPGBOT](https://rpgbot.net/p2/characters/remastered-arcane-spell-list)
- **Dizzying Colors (2 stars):** can blind and stun a small group, but the magus's DCs make that unlikely.

**Rank 2**
- **Invisibility (3 stars; 4 stars heightened to rank 4):** strong for scouting and escape. At rank 4 it becomes a top defensive option. RPGBOT: "Absolutely essential," and the rank 4 version lets you take hostile actions without ending it — [RPGBOT](https://rpgbot.net/p2/characters/remastered-arcane-spell-list)
- **Acid Grip (3 stars):** damage plus a speed debuff and repositioning, even when the target succeeds its Fortitude save, when used with Spellstrike. RPGBOT: weak initial damage, decent persistent damage, strong forced movement — [RPGBOT](https://rpgbot.net/p2/characters/remastered-arcane-spell-list)
- **Blazing Bolt (2 stars):** wasteful with a single Spellstrike, better with Spell Swipe or Split Spellstrike. RPGBOT (for casters): "Great damage" — [RPGBOT](https://rpgbot.net/p2/characters/remastered-arcane-spell-list)
- **Laughing Fit (1 star):** sustaining costs actions the magus needs for Spellstrike. RPGBOT, for casters, rates it a strong low-level option: slowed 1 with no incapacitation trait — [RPGBOT](https://rpgbot.net/p2/characters/remastered-arcane-spell-list)
- RPGBOT also rates Dispel Magic "absolutely essential" and Blur effective at any level (20% miss chance) — [RPGBOT](https://rpgbot.net/p2/characters/remastered-arcane-spell-list). Blur is also Laughing Shadow's level 7 studious spell (see Q5).

**Rank 3**
- **Haste (4 stars):** an extra Stride or Strike, ideal for Spellstrike. At rank 7 it can buff the whole party. MM also rates Potion of Quickness 4 stars as "Another source of haste!"
- **Cave Fangs (3 stars):** damage plus difficult terrain, so it doubles as crowd control.
- **Fireball (2 stars):** reliable for ranged spellstrikers with Expansive Spellstrike, but melee magi risk hitting themselves.
- **Lightning Bolt (2 stars):** long line, Reflex save. Works better with Expansive Spellstrike than Fireball because there's no self-harm risk.
- **Blindness (2 stars):** the incapacitation trait and weak DCs limit it. **Paralyze (2 stars; 3 stars at rank 7 with more targets):** needs a failed save.
- **Slow (2 stars):** the excerpt cut off before MM's reasoning.

**Rank 4**
- **Fly (4 stars):** flight at land Speed for five minutes. Staying airborne costs an action each turn, but three-dimensional movement is worth it. Lasts an hour at rank 7.
- **Translocate (3 stars):** "the most accessible teleport in the game," 120 ft. Being one use keeps it from a higher rating, but the rank 5 version is rated higher as a dependable escape. It is also Laughing Shadow's level 13 studious spell.
- **Containment (2 stars):** walls off enemies from squishier allies, but needs a creature target and the Reflex save interacts oddly with force constructs.
- **Howling Blizzard (2 stars):** a decent cone of cold, extendable to a burst with a third action, but limited by the Reflex save. MM lists it under rank 4; I didn't check its rank on AoN.

**Rank 5**
- **Wall of Stone (4 stars):** "among the best spells a Magus can use." Flexible (walls, stairs, bridges) and durable.
- **Glacial Skewer (4 stars):** less damage than Hydraulic Push but stronger knockback. Pushing a target into an obstacle can pin it.
- **Weight of the World (3 stars):** can stun even on a success, and adds bleed and dazzled. Low damage, but can cost enemies many actions.

**Rank 6**
- **Disintegrate (4 stars):** damage well above its rank, and a critical hit makes the target's save one step worse. Pair with Sure Strike.
- **Chain Lightning (2 stars):** hits enemies but not allies, but ends early if any target critically succeeds, a real risk at magus DC.
- **Petrify (2 stars):** essentially Lignify with stone, so it relies on a failed save.

**Rank 7**
- **True Target (4 stars):** Sure Strike for up to four creatures, ideally you plus three offensive allies.
- **Frigid Flurry (3 stars):** a respectable line of damage that also moves you to the far end of the line, an edge over other blasts.
- **Eclipse Burst (2 stars):** hits living creatures hard, but only the cold damage affects undead. Large burst, so watch allies.

**Rank 8**
- **Disappearance (4 stars):** invisible to every sense, with the duration of regular invisibility.
- **Falling Sky (3 stars):** grounds and damages flyers in a large area, and knocks prone even on a success.
- **Arctic Rift (2 stars):** a line of cold with control only on failure or critical failure, so best against weaker foes.
- **Quandary (1 star):** needs sustaining, which costs Spellstrike actions.

**Rank 9**
- **Foresight (4 stars):** "Whether you use it on yourself or on another teammate this is a great defensive buff."
- **Falling Stars (2 stars):** "still a blast spell and your spell DC holds it back from its full potential."
- **Phantasmagoria (2 stars):** "your spell DC does mean that you're unlikely to make the confusion effect land."
- **Implosion (1 star):** "If it were just a blast that hit everyone at once this would be a higher rating but alas."
- **Massacre (1 star):** "a gamble that your Spell DC isn't really up for."

### Inferences
- **Defence coverage for Cyrus by payload:**
  - AC (attack spells, using his strong weapon roll): Gouging Claw, Ignition, Needle Darts, Disintegrate (with Sure Strike or True Target).
  - Reflex: Thunderstrike, Lightning Bolt, Fireball, Frigid Flurry, Howling Blizzard, Chain Lightning.
  - Fortitude: Frostbite, Acid Grip, Glacial Skewer, Petrify.
  - Will: Fear, Dizzying Colors, Phantasmagoria — mostly low-rated because of DC.
  
  Since Will spells consistently score low with magus DC, Will coverage may be better left to the Cleric, Druid and Animist.
- **Slot priorities (ratings plus the few-slots structure):** put buffs and non-DC spells in top slots (Sure Strike, Haste, rank 4 Invisibility, Fly, True Target, Disappearance, Foresight), plus one Expansive/Sprinting line payload (Lightning Bolt, then Frigid Flurry, then Arctic Rift) and one or two control payloads (Thunderstrike, Acid Grip, Glacial Skewer, Weight of the World, Cave Fangs). Utility that depends on circumstances (Translocate, Wall of Stone, Containment, Dispel Magic, See the Unseen, Revealing Light) fits scrolls well. This is inferred from MM's "just in case" scroll advice.
- **For Cyrus specifically:** he loses the free-hand damage bonus but keeps the Laughing Shadow Speed bonus. So Tailwind and Fleet Step are lower priority, and the Speed bonus plus Sprinting Spellstrike already give him mobility.

### Gaps
- I found no MM or RPGBOT ratings for **Befuddle, Revealing Light** (RPGBOT only notes it as the better dazzled option), **Time Jump, Vision of Death, See the Unseen**, or MM's full **Slow** entry.
- I didn't confirm on AoN the remaster names, ranks or traditions for Howling Blizzard, Glacial Skewer, Frigid Flurry, Weight of the World, Arctic Rift, Quandary, Eclipse Burst, Falling Sky or Falling Stars. The ratings above come from MM.
- MM's "Falling Sky" (rank 8) and the brief's "Falling Stars" (rank 9) are different spells. MM gives Falling Stars 2 stars.
- RPGBOT's rank 3+ section wasn't reached; that page is marked incomplete anyway.

## Q3. Best line, cone and burst spells with Expansive Spellstrike and Sprinting Spellstrike, and rules debates

### Takeaway
The remaster Expansive Spellstrike text settles the old reach/cone question: if you aren't adjacent to the target (for example with a reach weapon), you pick any square adjacent to the target as the origin of a cone or line. Bursts centre on a corner of the target's square. Sprinting Spellstrike needs a line spell and lets you Stride straight up to the line's length without triggering reactions; with Expansive, the line follows your movement path. For a melee reach magus, the consensus favours lines (Lightning Bolt, Frigid Flurry, Arctic Rift) over bursts like Fireball, which risk hitting yourself.

### Cited Findings
- **Expansive Spellstrike (Feat 2, Impossible Magic pg. 17):** with a burst, cone or line spell, the area is created around the target, and the Strike still targets only one creature. The target must be inside the area. It does nothing if the spell was disrupted, for example on a critical failure — [AoN Expansive Spellstrike](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?ID=9047)
  - Bursts: centre on any corner of the target's square, or any square of a Large or larger target — [AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?ID=9047)
  - Cones and lines start from you and must include the target. "If you aren't adjacent to the target, as with a reach weapon or starlit span, you choose any square adjacent to the target as the origin" — [AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?ID=9047)
  - All creatures in the area are affected normally, so allies can be hit — [AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?ID=9047)
- **Sprinting Spellstrike (Feat 12, 2 actions, Impossible Magic pg. 24):** requires a charged Spellstrike. You cast a line-area spell as part of a Spellstrike, and before the Strike you Stride in a straight line up to the spell's line length. You can pass through enemies without triggering reactions, and may use Burrow, Climb, Fly or Swim Speed instead — [AoN Sprinting Spellstrike](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?ID=9082)
- AoN cross-references Expansive Spellstrike, which "changes the spell's area to use the line of your movement instead of the normal line method" — [AoN Sprinting Spellstrike](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?ID=9082). MM agrees: "With Expansive Spellstrike, the line becomes the spell's area" — [Martial Magicks](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/d/1E_MwD2ewUIRcb29tr0KXUBfCmXbBNe_2im4-5Q6fRmI/mobilebasic)
- MM build ratings:
  - Expansive Spellstrike: Starlit Span 3 stars, Volatile Spark 3 stars, other builds 2 stars. MM notes burst spells can hit you.
  - Sprinting Spellstrike: Melee Magus Builds 3 stars.
  - Spell Swipe (level 8): 1 to 2 stars depending on build. Split Spellstrike (Twofold Tine, 10): 3 stars.
  
  [Martial Magicks](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/d/1E_MwD2ewUIRcb29tr0KXUBfCmXbBNe_2im4-5Q6fRmI/mobilebasic)
- MM on specific spells with Expansive:
  - Fireball and Lightning Bolt are the main options. Lightning Bolt has no self-harm risk, while Fireball endangers melee magi.
  - Breathe Fire is worth it only in tight spaces with grouped enemies.
  - Timber (1 star) works with Sprinting Spellstrike (15 ft move) but is still weak.
  - Frigid Flurry's movement to the far end of the line gives it an edge over other blasts.
  - Arctic Rift is a cold line.
  - Howling Blizzard is a cone, and its 3-action burst can't be Spellstruck because 3-action versions aren't allowed.
  
  [Martial Magicks](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/d/1E_MwD2ewUIRcb29tr0KXUBfCmXbBNe_2im4-5Q6fRmI/mobilebasic)

### Inferences
- **Reach plus cones/lines:** Cyrus attacks from 10 ft, so the cone or line origin moves to a square next to the target. A cone then extends away from that square rather than from Cyrus. With careful placement he can avoid catching himself, which is harder with a large burst like Fireball (20 ft radius centred on the target's corner, so a 10 ft reach attacker is inside it).
- **Sprinting Spellstrike line candidates, by rank:** Lightning Bolt (3), Frigid Flurry (7, MM 3 stars), Arctic Rift (8). Frigid Flurry's own "move to the end of the line" effect may overlap or interact with Sprinting's Stride. I found no ruling on this, so flag it for the GM.
- **Debates still open:**
  - Whether Sprinting Spellstrike plus Expansive lets the line extend past the target.
  - How Frigid Flurry's built-in movement stacks with Sprinting's Stride.
  - Whether a Strike that misses still creates the area for save spells. By the Spellstrike rule, a miss that isn't critical still forces saves, and Expansive only fails on disruption, so the area probably still happens. That is my inference, not a ruling.

### Gaps
- I didn't find Reddit or Paizo forum threads debating the Impossible Magic wording of Sprinting or Expansive Spellstrike, or Frigid Flurry interactions. A Paizo thread on legacy Sprinting Spellstrike appeared in search but I didn't read it: [paizo.com/threads/rzs2oo2z](https://paizo.com/threads/rzs2oo2z).
- I didn't confirm the remaster areas of Lightning Bolt, Frigid Flurry or Arctic Rift (line lengths) on AoN.

## Q4. Wands vs scrolls vs staves for a magus with few slots per rank

### Takeaway
Consensus: "Wands are essentially spell slots that cost money." Put long-duration buffs you'd use every day on wands; Tailwind is the classic, with Haste and Invisibility also suggested. Scrolls are for "just in case" utility, and any arcane scroll is usable and learnable. Staves are prized for magi when they carry Sure Strike (Staff of the Unblinking Eye) or a magus-tailored list (Spellstriker's Staff). Striker's Scroll (casting a scroll as your Spellstrike) costs too much for regular use without the Scroll Trickster archetype or a generous GM.

### Cited Findings
- **Slot structure (legacy page; remaster not confirmed in my sources):** no more than two slots of your highest rank, plus two of the rank below once you can cast rank 2+. Cantrips 5 per day. 9th-rank spells at level 17 — [AoN legacy Magus](https://2e.aonprd.com/Classes.aspx?ID=67). MM: the magus "can never gain 10th-rank spells" — [Martial Magicks](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/d/1E_MwD2ewUIRcb29tr0KXUBfCmXbBNe_2im4-5Q6fRmI/mobilebasic)
- **Wands (MM):** "Wands are essentially spell slots that cost money." Use them for spells with "long durations that you are likely to use every single day." Specialty wands are less desirable because they emulate Spellshape feats or occupy a hand — [Martial Magicks](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/d/1E_MwD2ewUIRcb29tr0KXUBfCmXbBNe_2im4-5Q6fRmI/mobilebasic)
- Wand candidates in MM: rank 2 Tailwind, rank 5 Darkvision, Haste, Invisibility. Elsewhere it mentions Energy Aegis, Planar Palace and Hidden Mind for wands or scrolls. ### Inferences
- **Suggested split for Cyrus:**
  - **Wands:** Tailwind rank 2 (lower value for him because of the Laughing Shadow Speed bonus), See the Unseen, Invisibility, Haste rank 3, later rank 4 Invisibility.
  - **Scrolls:** Translocate (before level 13 studious access), Dispel Magic, Wall of Stone, Containment, Revealing Light, Fly (before relying on slots), plus situational area payloads. Payload scrolls only become Spellstrikes through Striker's Scroll.
  - **Staff:** Spellstriker's Staff or Staff of the Unblinking Eye for Sure Strike every day.
- Reading the ratings together, buffs and utility spells that ignore the magus's DC are the best use of consumables. That keeps the few top slots free for Haste, Sure Strike or True Target and one big Spellstrike payload.

## Q5. Laughing Shadow studious spells (remaster) and their ratings

### Takeaway
The remaster Laughing Shadow (Impossible Magic pg. 13) gives the conflux spell Dimensional Assault and the studious spells Blur (level 7), Ghostly Weapon (level 11) and Translocate (level 13). MM rates the study 4 stars. Blur and Translocate are well regarded (Blur effective at any level, Translocate 3 stars and higher at rank 5). For Cyrus, the study's free-hand damage clause is the bigger problem than the spell list.

### Cited Findings
- Remaster Laughing Shadow:
  - Arcane Cascade grants +5 ft status bonus to Speeds (+10 ft unarmored).
  - Striking an off-guard creature with a free hand raises the stance's extra damage to 3 (5 with weapon specialization, 7 with greater). "The other hand must be completely free."
  - Conflux spell: Dimensional Assault.
  - Studious spells: Blur (7th), Ghostly Weapon (11th), Translocate (13th).
  
  [AoN Laughing Shadow](https://2e.aonprd.com/HybridStudies.aspx?ID=11)
- MM rates Laughing Shadow 4 stars and lists the same conflux and studious spells. Its unique feats are Distracting Spellstrike (4th) and Dimensional Disappearance (10th) — [Martial Magicks](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/d/1E_MwD2ewUIRcb29tr0KXUBfCmXbBNe_2im4-5Q6fRmI/mobilebasic)
- Conflux spells recharge Spellstrike when cast, which MM calls "MASSIVELY important" to the action rotation — [Martial Magicks](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/d/1E_MwD2ewUIRcb29tr0KXUBfCmXbBNe_2im4-5Q6fRmI/mobilebasic); also [AoN Spellstrike](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=4260)
- Legacy (Secrets of Magic) studious spells, for comparison: two rank 2 slots at level 7 for Gecko Grip, Sure Strike, Water Breathing plus the hybrid spell; Haste added at 11; Fly at 13 — [AoN legacy Magus](https://2e.aonprd.com/Classes.aspx?ID=67)
- Ratings:
  - Translocate: 3 stars (higher at rank 5) — [Martial Magicks](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/d/1E_MwD2ewUIRcb29tr0KXUBfCmXbBNe_2im4-5Q6fRmI/mobilebasic)
  - Blur: effective at any level (20% miss chance) — [RPGBOT](https://rpgbot.net/p2/characters/remastered-arcane-spell-list)
  - MM rates Warp Step and Tailwind lower for Laughing Shadows because of the built-in Speed bonus, and Thicket of Knives 2 stars for feinting — [Martial Magicks](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/d/1E_MwD2ewUIRcb29tr0KXUBfCmXbBNe_2im4-5Q6fRmI/mobilebasic)

### Inferences
- Translocate as a level 13 studious spell means Cyrus shouldn't spend a scroll or main slot on it after 13. Before then it's a good scroll.
- Ghostly Weapon at 11 is mostly redundant once his weapon carries property runes. Its value would be against incorporeal foes, but I found no rating for it.
- The remaster study seems to replace the legacy general studious list (Sure Strike, Haste, Fly) with three study-specific spells. If so, Sure Strike and Haste must come from main slots, wands or staves, which raises the value of the Spellstriker's Staff, Staff of the Unblinking Eye and Ring of Wizardry. Whether the remaster keeps a general studious list as well isn't confirmed.
