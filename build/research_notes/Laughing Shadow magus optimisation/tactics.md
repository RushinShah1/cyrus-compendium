# Laughing Shadow Magus Combat Play (PF2e Remaster): Turn Structure, Mobility, Rules Interactions, Teamwork

IMPORTANT CONTEXT FOR THE WRITER: The remastered magus is in **Impossible Magic** (2026), not Player Core 2. Archives of Nethys (AoN) now redirects magus pages to the Impossible Magic versions and labels the Secrets of Magic text as Legacy. Several assumptions in the brief are out of date. **Expansive Spellstrike is now a level-2 feat, Running Recharge is level 4, Arcane Shroud is level 14 and Hasted Assault is level 14.** Arcane Cascade's damage no longer depends on the spell. The remastered Spellstrike explicitly says save spells force a save even if the Strike misses. Community discussion of the *remastered* magus was thin: the Paizo forum Impossible Magic threads would not load, and almost no remaster-era Reddit tactics threads turned up. Most "how experienced players run it" material below comes from legacy-era forum threads. Treat it as applicable only where the rules are unchanged.

## 1. Remaster core rules: Spellstrike, Arcane Cascade, Laughing Shadow, Dimensional Assault, Force Fang, Magus's Analysis

### Takeaway
Remaster Spellstrike is still 2 actions and counts as two attacks for MAP. It recharges with 1 concentrate action or any conflux spell of 1+ actions. Save spells now force the save regardless of the Strike result, unless the Strike critically fails. Arcane Cascade is now a flat +1/+2/+3 force damage (retypeable), plus the arcane trait and spell resistance. Laughing Shadow raises that to 3/5/7 against off-guard targets, but only with a completely free hand. The usual loop is still "Spellstrike → conflux spell (Dimensional Assault) or recharge action → Spellstrike". Running Recharge (level 4) makes recharge-plus-move a single action.

### Cited Findings
**Spellstrike (Impossible Magic pg. 11)**
- 2 actions, Magus trait, frequency "until recharged". You cast a 1- or 2-action spell that needs a spell attack roll or a saving throw and load it into a melee Strike with a weapon or unarmed attack. The Strike gains the arcane trait. — [AoN Magus class](https://2e.aonprd.com/Classes.aspx?ID=74)
- MAP: Spellstrike "counts as two attacks" for MAP, but the penalty applies only after the Spellstrike is complete. — [AoN Magus class](https://2e.aonprd.com/Classes.aspx?ID=74)
- Attack-roll spells: the Strike's result determines the outcome for both the Strike and the spell. Save spells: the target saves normally *regardless of the Strike result*, unless the Strike critically fails, which disrupts the spell. — [AoN Magus class](https://2e.aonprd.com/Classes.aspx?ID=74)
- Strike and spell damage are combined for resistances and weaknesses. — [AoN Magus class](https://2e.aonprd.com/Classes.aspx?ID=74)
- Recharge: a single concentrate action. Casting a conflux spell that takes at least 1 action also recharges it. Other focus spells do not. — [AoN Magus class](https://2e.aonprd.com/Classes.aspx?ID=74)
- Area spells affect only the Strike's target unless a feat (e.g., Expansive Spellstrike) says otherwise. The spell uses your weapon's reach. An invalid target wastes the spell. If the target is immune to the Strike but not the spell, the spell still applies. For variable-action spells you pick the 1- or 2-action version. Free-action, reaction and 3+-action spells don't qualify. Spellshape generally can't be used with Spellstrike. — [AoN Magus class](https://2e.aonprd.com/Classes.aspx?ID=74)
- Double Spellstrike (level 19): once per day, a charged Spellstrike can carry two qualifying spells with combined rank ≤10. — [AoN Magus class](https://2e.aonprd.com/Classes.aspx?ID=74)

**Arcane Cascade (Impossible Magic pg. 12)**
- 1 action, traits Concentrate, Magus, Stance. In the stance, melee Strikes gain the arcane trait and deal 1 extra force damage, and you gain resistance 1 to spell damage. Both rise to 2 at weapon specialization (level 7) and 3 at greater weapon specialization (level 15). If you Cast a Spell and then enter Arcane Cascade on the same turn, the extra damage can become any one damage type that spell could deal. — [AoN Arcane Cascade](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=4261)
- The stance ends if you're knocked out, its requirements are violated, the encounter ends, or you take another stance action. After any stance action you can't take another for 1 round. — [AoN Magus class](https://2e.aonprd.com/Classes.aspx?ID=74)
- Studious spells (level 7+): casting a studious spell lets you use Arcane Cascade as a free action as your next action that turn. The base list is gecko grip plus one hybrid-study spell, adding haste and another study spell at 11, and fly and another at 13. — [AoN Magus class](https://2e.aonprd.com/Classes.aspx?ID=74)

**Laughing Shadow (Impossible Magic pg. 13)**
- In Arcane Cascade: +5-foot status bonus to Speeds, or +10 feet if unarmored. Striking an off-guard creature with a free hand raises the stance's extra damage to 3 (5 at weapon specialization, 7 at greater weapon specialization). "You must have your other hand completely free". A free-hand weapon or any item in that hand turns the bonus off. Conflux spell: Dimensional Assault. — [AoN Laughing Shadow (remaster)](https://2e.aonprd.com/HybridStudies.aspx?ID=2)
- Legacy (Secrets of Magic pg. 39) wording was essentially the same: +5/+10 status Speed, 3/5/7 against flat-footed creatures with a free hand, and the same "completely free" clause. Legacy studious spells were Blur (7th), Shift Blame (11th) and Translocate (13th). — [AoN Laughing Shadow (legacy)](https://2e.aonprd.com/HybridStudies.aspx?ID=2&NoRedirect=1)

**Dimensional Assault (Impossible Magic pg. 179)**
- Focus 1, one action. Traits: Uncommon, Concentrate, Focus, Magus, Teleportation. Range: half your Speed. You teleport to a visible space within range that is within reach of an enemy, then make a melee Strike against that enemy. It is disrupted if you try to bring another creature along, including one in an extradimensional container. **Heightened (5th): range equals your Speed.** — [AoN Dimensional Assault](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=2877)
- Legacy (Secrets of Magic pg. 143): one action, conjuration/teleportation, half Speed, teleport within reach of a creature and then a melee Strike. The AoN summary listed no heightened entry. — [AoN Dimensional Assault (legacy)](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=1037&NoRedirect=1)

**Force Fang / Magus's Analysis**
- Not found in any remaster source fetched (see Gaps).

**Other relevant remaster feats (Impossible Magic)**
- **Running Recharge (Feat 4, 1 action, Concentrate):** recharge your Spellstrike, then Stride up to half your Speed or Step. — [AoN Running Recharge](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?ID=9057)
- **Rapid Recharge (Feat 10, free action, 1/day):** recharge your Spellstrike. — [AoN Rapid Recharge](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?ID=9077)
- **Spell Retaliation (Feat 6, free action):** trigger is succeeding at a save against an enemy spell, or an enemy failing a spell attack against you, while your Spellstrike isn't charged. Effect: recharge Spellstrike. — [AoN Spell Retaliation](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?ID=9065)
- **Spell Crash (Feat 6, 2 actions, Arcane/Force):** requires a charged Spellstrike and a prepared slot. You expend the slot for a 20-ft emanation, 30-ft cone or 60-ft line dealing 1d10 force per spell rank (basic Reflex vs spell DC). **If your weapon has the trip trait, creatures that fail fall prone.** With shove, they're pushed 10 ft. Damage can be retyped to the expended spell's type. Spellstrike is no longer charged afterwards. — [AoN Spell Crash](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?ID=9064)
- **Spell Swipe (Feat 8, 3 actions):** Spellstrike two adjacent creatures within melee reach, with separate Strikes. It counts as two attacks for MAP. It interacts with Expansive Spellstrike. — [AoN Spell Swipe](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?ID=9069)
- **Standby Spell (Feat 8):** cast one chosen Spellstrike-eligible spell from your spellbook spontaneously using a slot. — [AoN Standby Spell](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?ID=9070)
- **Fused Staff (Feat 8, Arcane):** fuse a magic staff into your weapon (daily prep or 10 min). You gain the staff's activations and spells, the two share fundamental runes (use the higher potency and striking), and the staff's property runes are suppressed. — [AoN Fused Staff](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?ID=9067)
- The Impossible Magic sample "Arcane Fist" (Laughing Shadow) is Dex-first with unarmed attacks. It lists Arcane Fists (1), Distracting Spellstrike (4), Spell Swipe (6) and Dimensional Disappearance (8). The level numbers are as AoN shows them, though the Dimensional Disappearance feat page says level 10. — [AoN Magus class samples](https://2e.aonprd.com/ClassSamples.aspx?Class=74)
- Commentary: RPGBOT's podcast notes say the magus gets "More spell slots and dramatically improved action economy" and that "Spellstrike finally feels like the centerpiece of the class." — [RPGBOT podcast S6E104](https://rpgbot.net/pf2e-impossible-magic-review-pt-1-finally-the-summoner-reads-its-own-job-description-rpgbot-podcast-s6e104/). Know Direction says the extra slots are "about flexibility" and calls the redesign "far healthier" than the cantrip-focused min-maxing some legacy players fell into. — [Know Direction, Investing In: Impossible Magic](https://knowdirectionpodcast.com/2026/07/investing-in-impossible-magic/)

**Legacy-era turn patterns (Paizo forums, "Laughing Shadow Magus in combat confuses me")**
- Kyrone: round 1 is usually Stride + Spellstrike. Round 2 is Dimensional Assault + Arcane Cascade, with 1 action left over (e.g., Shield). — [Paizo forums](https://paizo.com/threads/rzs2lqoc)
- Kyrone: because Dimensional Assault recharges Spellstrike, you usually cast it *after* Spellstrike. By then you're often already adjacent, so its movement goes unused unless you need to switch targets. — [Paizo forums](https://paizo.com/threads/rzs2lqoc)
- Another poster: if you Spellstruck last turn, you may be standing next to a dead enemy. Dimensional Assault's reposition + Strike + recharge in one action is very valuable. — [Paizo forums](https://paizo.com/threads/rzs2lqoc)
- Kyrone: hit one enemy with a persistent-damage Spellstrike, then teleport to a new target next round while the damage ticks. Use Dimensional Assault to flank large creatures, avoid creatures with Attacks of Opportunity, escape a grapple (it had only a verbal component in legacy) and skip difficult terrain. — [Paizo forums](https://paizo.com/threads/rzs2lqoc)
- Openers suggested: buff + Arcane Cascade (Magic Weapon at levels 1–3, Haste from level 5), or a ranged cantrip + Cascade. — [Paizo forums](https://paizo.com/threads/rzs2lqoc)

### Inferences
- **Recharge cadence in the remaster.** "One Spellstrike every other turn" is the floor. With the free recharge from Dimensional Assault (while focus points last) or Running Recharge (1 action that includes movement), a Spellstrike every turn is common:
  - **T1:** Arcane Cascade (1) + Spellstrike (2).
  - **T2:** Dimensional Assault (1, Strike at MAP 0, recharges) + Spellstrike (2, at −5 MAP).
  - **T3:** Running Recharge (1) + Spellstrike (2, MAP 0).
  
  Remaster focus pools refill only 1 point per 10-minute Refocus ([AoN Magus class](https://2e.aonprd.com/Classes.aspx?ID=74)). Cyrus's pool will be 1 point until he gains a second focus spell, which on this plan means Hasted Assault at 14. So Dimensional Assault is roughly once per encounter, and Running Recharge carries the routine.
- **MAP ordering.** Spellstrike counts as two attacks, so any Strike, Trip or Dimensional Assault Strike after it is at −10 (−8 agile). Put the MAP-0 slot on whichever roll matters most. Usually that's the Spellstrike. If you need a Trip to set up off-guard first, accept that the Spellstrike will be at −5.
- **Save spells.** Save spells now force a save even on a missed Strike, so Spellstrike with a save spell is close to "cast the spell plus get a free Strike". The main risk is a critical-failure Strike.
- **Arcane Cascade retyping.** Casting a spell and *then* entering Cascade on the same turn lets you retype the bonus to hit a weakness. A buff-spell + Cascade opener (e.g., a studious spell giving a free Cascade at 7+) does both at once.

### Gaps
- No remaster text was found for Force Fang or Magus's Analysis. They may not exist in Impossible Magic, may be under different names, or may simply not have surfaced. The brief's assumption should be verified in the book.
- Remaster Laughing Shadow studious spells were not shown on the AoN page fetched. The legacy ones were Blur, Shift Blame and Translocate.
- I could not confirm how the legacy Spellstrike handled save spells on a miss, so the exact remaster "change" here isn't verified. Only the remaster text is confirmed.
- The exact remaster slot counts behind "more spell slots" weren't obtained. AoN shows one 1st-rank slot at level 1.
- Distracting Spellstrike (Feat 4, in the sample build) was not fetched.

## 2. Dimensional Assault positioning and Dimensional Disappearance

### Takeaway
Dimensional Assault teleports up to half your Speed (your full Speed from 5th rank, i.e. magus level 9), and Laughing Shadow's Cascade speed bonus extends that range. You must land within reach of an enemy. With a 10-ft reach weapon you can land 10 ft away, outside many enemies' natural reach, and Strike at MAP 0. Dimensional Disappearance (Feat 10) turns you invisible at the end of the teleport, makes the Strike optional, and ends the invisibility after the Strike if you take it.

### Cited Findings
- Range is half Speed, raised to Speed at 5th rank. You must land in a visible space within reach of an enemy, then make a melee Strike. It has the Concentrate and Teleportation traits. — [AoN Dimensional Assault](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=2877)
- Focus spells auto-heighten to half your level rounded up, so 5th rank arrives at level 9. — [AoN Magus class](https://2e.aonprd.com/Classes.aspx?ID=74)
- Laughing Shadow gives +5 ft status Speed in Cascade, or +10 if unarmored. — [AoN Laughing Shadow](https://2e.aonprd.com/HybridStudies.aspx?ID=2)
- **Dimensional Disappearance (Feat 10, prereq: dimensional assault and Laughing Shadow):** "When you cast dimensional assault, you're affected by an invisibility spell at the end of the teleport." "You can choose not to make the Strike that is normally part of dimensional assault." "If you do make the Strike, your invisibility ends after the Strike, as normal for invisibility." — [AoN Dimensional Disappearance](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?ID=9073)
- Legacy forum advice for the level-10 feat: cast Dimensional Assault *before* Spellstrike to set up the target as flat-footed, or *after* it to teleport next to an ally and become invisible. — [Paizo forums](https://paizo.com/threads/rzs2lqoc)
- Use Dimensional Assault to flank, or to escape being flanked, as the fight shifts. — [Paizo forums](https://paizo.com/threads/rzs2lqoc)

### Inferences
- **Dimensional Disappearance → off-guard → Laughing Shadow damage.** The Strike made while invisible should be against a target that can't see Cyrus. In general PF2e rules, a creature you are hidden from or undetected by is off-guard to you. I didn't re-verify this rule this session. If it holds, the Dimensional Assault Strike after Dimensional Disappearance triggers the Laughing Shadow off-guard bonus (3/5/7) with no ally setup.
- **Best ordering at level 10+:**
  - **Opener, if Spellstrike is charged:** Dimensional Assault (invisible, Strike at MAP 0 against an off-guard target), then Spellstrike at −5. Invisibility ends after the first Strike, so the Spellstrike is against a seeing target unless it's otherwise off-guard. Dimensional Assault also wastes its recharge here, because Spellstrike was already charged.
  - **Alternative:** Spellstrike, then Dimensional Assault *skipping the Strike* (which would be at −10 anyway) to vanish and reposition invisibly. This also recharges Spellstrike for next turn. It's an efficient defensive pattern.
- **Reach geometry.** With a 10-ft reach weapon, "within reach of an enemy" lets Cyrus land 10 ft from the target. That's outside a 5-ft-reach enemy's Reactive Strike range, and in a square chosen to flank with the Champion (flanking requires both flankers to be able to act against the target, so reach counts).
- **Dimensional Assault vs Reactive Strike.** Remaster Dimensional Assault has Concentrate, not Manipulate, so in general rules it shouldn't trigger Reactive Strike, which typically triggers on manipulate or move actions (not verified this session). Teleportation also isn't a Stride.
- **Range scaling.** At level 9+ with Cascade active and unarmored (+10 status), the teleport reaches the full boosted Speed. Fleet Step from Arcane Shroud would be a larger status bonus, but status bonuses don't stack, so it replaces Laughing Shadow's rather than adding to it.

### Gaps
- Remaster community threads on Dimensional Disappearance timing (e.g., whether the GM treats the target as off-guard for the Strike) weren't found. The invisibility→off-guard rule is general rules knowledge, not confirmed in sources fetched here.
- The remaster wording on whether teleportation triggers movement-based reactions wasn't fetched.

## 3. Sprinting Spellstrike and Expansive Spellstrike: wording, rulings, combos

### Takeaway
Remaster **Expansive Spellstrike is a level-2 feat**. Bursts center on any corner of the target's square (or of any square of a Large+ creature's space). Cones and lines come from you, or from any square adjacent to the target if you're not adjacent, which matters for reach weapons. It does nothing if the Strike critically fails. **Sprinting Spellstrike (Feat 12, 2 actions)** needs a line spell: you Stride in a straight line up to the line's length before the Strike, through enemies and without triggering reactions. With Expansive Spellstrike, the line follows your path of movement.

### Cited Findings
- **Expansive Spellstrike (Feat 2, Impossible Magic pg. 17):** with a burst, cone or line spell, the spell creates its area instead of affecting only the target, and the target must be inside the area. No effect if the spell was disrupted (e.g., a critical-failure Strike). It affects every creature in the area, but the Strike targets only one.
  - Burst: centered on any corner you choose of the target's square. For a Large or larger creature, any corner of any square in its space.
  - Cone or line: emits from you and must include the target. If you aren't adjacent (e.g., reach weapon or starlit span), choose any square adjacent to the target as the source.
  - Emanations aren't covered.
  
  — [AoN Expansive Spellstrike](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?ID=9047)
- **Legacy difference:** the legacy version used the corner closest to the target's centre for a Large+ creature, with your choice among ties. The remaster allows any corner of any occupied square. — [AoN Expansive Spellstrike (legacy)](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?ID=2849&NoRedirect=1)
- **Sprinting Spellstrike (Feat 12, 2 actions, Impossible Magic pg. 24):** requires a charged Spellstrike. You Spellstrike with a line-area spell. Before the Strike, you Stride in a straight line up to the line's length, can move through enemies, and the movement doesn't trigger reactions. You can use a Burrow, Climb, Fly or Swim Speed instead. Special: with Expansive Spellstrike, "the spell's area uses the line of your movement instead of the normal method of determining the line." — [AoN Sprinting Spellstrike](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?ID=9082)
- **Forum explanations of Expansive Spellstrike:**
  - The Strike targets one creature, but the area spell hits everything in its area. A burst still has its full radius, so an adjacent magus may be inside their own Fireball (Taja the Barbarian and others).
  - Ascalaphus: with reach weapons or starlit span you can originate cones and lines from a square adjacent to the target, which makes them much easier to aim. Posters noted it lets you aim around corners.
  - A Shadow Lodge poster suggested a grid trick to keep yourself out of the burst when using reach against Huge or Gargantuan creatures, framed as a loophole.
  
  — [Paizo forums, "Please Explain Expansive Spellstrike"](https://paizo.com/threads/rzs7iqcp)
- **Spell Swipe + Expansive:** pick one of the two targets to set the area. — [AoN Spell Swipe](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?ID=9069)
- **Spell Crash (Feat 6)** offers a non-Spellstrike area option: a 30-ft cone or 60-ft line from your weapon, with prone on a failed save if the weapon has trip. — [AoN Spell Crash](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?ID=9064)

### Inferences
- **Reach + Expansive is a strong pairing for Cyrus.** Because a reach magus picks the cone or line origin from any square adjacent to the target, he can angle cones (e.g., Frigid Flurry-type spells) and lines away from the Champion in melee. A ground-placed area like Blazing Fissure would follow the same line rules if it's a line spell (not verified).
- **Bursts.** Centered on a target's corner with Cyrus 10 ft away, a 5-ft burst won't include him, but a 10-ft+ burst might. The remaster "any corner of any square" for big creatures gives much more freedom to place bursts away from allies.
- **Sprinting Spellstrike + Lightning Bolt.** Run up to the line's length through the enemy line without provoking, then Strike. With Expansive, the bolt follows your path, hitting everyone you passed through. It's the signature engage/disengage move from 12.
  - It needs a charged Spellstrike, so pair it with Running Recharge or Dimensional Assault on the turn before.
  - Remaster save spells resolve even if the Strike misses, so the area damage is reliable unless the Strike critically fails.
- **Expansive and remaster saves.** Expansive area spells now deliver full-area damage whenever the Strike merely misses, which makes the feat strictly more reliable than in legacy (inference from the remaster Spellstrike save wording).

### Gaps
- No community ruling was found on whether Sprinting Spellstrike's movement can exceed your Speed when the line (e.g., a 120-ft Lightning Bolt) is longer. The text says "up to the line's length", but Stride is normally capped at Speed.
- Remaster area dimensions for Lightning Bolt, Blazing Fissure and Frigid Flurry, and whether each is a line or cone, weren't fetched.
- No YouTube (Rules Lawyer / How It's Played) remaster magus content was found.

## 4. Reach-weapon magus play: Reactive Strike, Trip, hand economy, the free-hand requirement

### Takeaway
RAW, Laughing Shadow's bonus damage needs the other hand "completely free", so a two-handed reach weapon wouldn't qualify. Cyrus's GM allowing it is a house rule. The trip trait combines with Athletics trips, and especially with Spell Crash (level 6), which knocks prone everything that fails the save in a cone, line or emanation. Fused Staff (8) is the main hand-economy fix for a two-hander who wants staff spells. Running Recharge is actually a level-4 feat, so taking it at 8 is fine but could come earlier.

### Cited Findings
- Laughing Shadow: "You must have your other hand completely free". A free-hand weapon or other item disables the bonus "even if you would normally be able to use the hand for other things." The legacy text is the same. — [AoN Laughing Shadow](https://2e.aonprd.com/HybridStudies.aspx?ID=2); [legacy](https://2e.aonprd.com/HybridStudies.aspx?ID=2&NoRedirect=1)
- Spellstrike delivers the spell with your weapon's reach. — [AoN Magus class](https://2e.aonprd.com/Classes.aspx?ID=74)
- Spell Crash: if your weapon has the trip trait, creatures that fail the basic Reflex save fall prone. — [AoN Spell Crash](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?ID=9064)
- Fused Staff: the fused weapon gains the staff's activations and spells. The staff's property runes are suppressed. — [AoN Fused Staff](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?ID=9067)
- Running Recharge: Feat 4, 1 action, recharge then Stride half Speed or Step. — [AoN Running Recharge](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?ID=9057)
- Hasted Assault (Feat 14) grants the *hasted assault* conflux spell: 1 action, Manipulate, 1-minute duration. You become quickened, with the extra action usable only for Stride or Strike, and you don't get the action on the turn you cast it. — [AoN Hasted Assault feat](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?ID=2878); [spell](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=1039)

### Inferences
- **The house rule.** Ask whether "free hand" for Laughing Shadow also lets him hold the weapon two-handed while using the hand for other things. The cleanest version: the bonus applies while the reach weapon is wielded two-handed and nothing else is held.
- **Hand economy (general rules knowledge, not verified this session).** Releasing a hand from a two-handed weapon is free, and regripping is a 1-action Interact. Drinking a potion or using a scroll therefore costs an extra action, which argues for Fused Staff (spells through the weapon) over scrolls.
- **Reactive Strike + 10-ft reach.** This covers a large zone (generic Reactive Strike triggers on manipulate or move actions within reach; I didn't verify whether the remaster magus list includes Reactive Strike). Reactive Strike is a Strike, not a Trip, so it can't trip on its own.
- **Level 6: Reactive Strike vs Spell Crash.** For a trip-weapon magus, Spell Crash (area prone) may beat Reactive Strike. It sets up off-guard for Laughing Shadow, gives everyone the prone attack bonus, and pairs well with the Champion and Druid.
- **Hasted Assault's Manipulate trait** means casting it within reach of an enemy with Reactive Strike may provoke. Cast it from outside reach, or as the opener before engaging. As a conflux spell it also recharges Spellstrike (it takes at least 1 action).

### Gaps
- Reactive Strike's availability and level on the remaster magus list wasn't confirmed. The AoN feat list didn't render.
- Retrieval Prism and Potion Patch rules weren't researched.
- No community examples of GMs ruling on two-handed Laughing Shadow were found.

## 5. Teamwork: Champion, Cleric, Druid, Animist; Trip and debuffs; Improvise Strategy; Bon Mot

### Takeaway
Laughing Shadow's extra damage only works against off-guard targets. The party's main job for Cyrus is generating off-guard: flanking with the Champion, prone from Trip or Spell Crash, Improvise Strategy, or invisibility from Dimensional Disappearance. Improvise Strategy is an **uncommon level-7 skill feat from Pathfinder #223: Hell's Destiny** that requires **master** Warfare Lore. Bon Mot (Player Core 2) lowers Will and Perception, which helps the casters (Cleric, Druid, Animist).

### Cited Findings
- **Improvise Strategy (Feat 7, 1 action):**
  - Traits: Uncommon, Bravado, Concentrate, General, Mental, Skill. Prerequisite: master in Warfare Lore. Source: Pathfinder #223: Hell's Destiny pg. 231.
  - You add the auditory or visual trait, then roll Warfare Lore against the Perception DC of a creature within 30 ft.
  - Critical success: your choice of clumsy 1, dazzled, off-guard or stupefied 1 until the end of your next turn, with no temporary immunity.
  - Success: the same, but the target becomes immune to the feat for 1 hour.
  - Critical failure: you are off-guard until your next turn.
  - The target can end it early with a concentrate action (auditory) or an Interact to rub its eyes (visual).
  
  — [AoN Improvise Strategy](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?ID=8982)
- **Bon Mot (Feat 1, 1 action):** Player Core 2 pg. 228. Diplomacy against Will DC, target within 30 ft.
  - Success: −2 status penalty to Perception and Will for 1 minute. Critical success: −3.
  - Critical failure: you take the penalty instead.
  - The target can end it with a retort (a concentrate action or a suitable skill action).
  - Traits include auditory, emotion, linguistic and mental.
  
  — [AoN Bon Mot](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?ID=2114)
- Spell Crash with a trip weapon knocks prone every creature that fails its Reflex save. — [AoN Spell Crash](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?ID=9064)
- Legacy advice: use Dimensional Assault to flank big creatures, and to escape flanks as positions shift. — [Paizo forums](https://paizo.com/threads/rzs2lqoc)

### Inferences
- **Champion.** The Champion is Cyrus's natural flanking partner. Each turn, use Dimensional Assault or a Stride to reach the square opposite the Champion; with reach that can be 10 ft away. Every Strike then triggers Laughing Shadow's 3/5/7 with no action spent on setup. The Champion's defensive reactions cover Cyrus, a medium-armor or unarmored magus with modest HP (8 + Con per level, [AoN Magus class](https://2e.aonprd.com/Classes.aspx?ID=74)).
- **Trip / prone (general rules knowledge, not re-verified).** Prone makes a target off-guard and penalises its attacks. Standing up costs it an action and is a move action, so it can provoke Reactive Strike from Cyrus's reach. Sequence: Trip (MAP 0) → Spellstrike (−5, target off-guard). Or let the Champion flank so the Trip isn't needed.
- **Improvise Strategy.** Warfare Lore is Int-based (as Lore skills generally are), which suits the magus's secondary attribute. Choose off-guard when no flank is available (it also lowers AC for the whole party), and stupefied 1 against enemy casters.
  - Watch the 1-hour immunity on a plain success: each enemy can usually be hit once per fight unless you critically succeed.
  - Master Warfare Lore by 7 requires spending skill increases on it at 3 and 7 (inference from standard proficiency progression).
- **Bon Mot.** It costs an action, and Cyrus's action budget is tight: Spellstrike is 2, plus recharge or Dimensional Assault. Bon Mot is most valuable on a turn where the Druid, Cleric or Animist will throw a Will save, or as a 3rd action after a Spellstrike when no good Strike remains (a Strike there would be at −10). With a Strength build, Charisma may be low, so expect modest odds.
- **Druid area spells.** Remaster Expansive Spellstrike lets Cyrus aim cones and lines from beside the target, keeping his own areas off allies and coordinating with the Druid's. Area prone from Spell Crash helps the Druid only indirectly, since prone doesn't affect Reflex saves (general rules knowledge).
- **Healing Cleric.** The Cleric's support makes the aggressive play (teleport deep, Spellstrike) sustainable. Dimensional Disappearance's no-Strike option is an escape tool when low.

## 6. Common mistakes and best practices (action economy)

### Takeaway
The biggest mistakes are attacking after a Spellstrike at −10 instead of using the 3rd action for recharge, movement, Trip setup or a skill action, and spending the turn's MAP-0 attack on a low-value Strike. Also: Spellstriking with spells that don't qualify, and forgetting that a critical-failure Strike wastes the spell (and its Expansive area). Cast directly when you need multiple targets without Expansive, spells of 3+ actions, buffs, or spells with neither an attack roll nor a save.

### Cited Findings
- Spellstrike counts as two attacks for MAP. — [AoN Magus class](https://2e.aonprd.com/Classes.aspx?ID=74)
- Spells that don't qualify: free-action, reaction and 3+-action spells, and spells with neither an attack roll nor a save. Spellshape generally doesn't work with Spellstrike. An invalid target wastes the spell. — [AoN Magus class](https://2e.aonprd.com/Classes.aspx?ID=74)
- A critical-failure Strike disrupts the spell, and Expansive Spellstrike then has no effect. — [AoN Magus class](https://2e.aonprd.com/Classes.aspx?ID=74); [AoN Expansive Spellstrike](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?ID=9047)
- After any stance action you can't take another stance action for 1 round, which matters for re-entering Cascade to trigger Arcane Shroud. — [AoN Magus class](https://2e.aonprd.com/Classes.aspx?ID=74)
- **Arcane Shroud (Feat 14, prereq Arcane Cascade):**
  - When you take the feat, choose 3 of false vitality, fleet step, invisibility, mountain resilience, resist energy and see the unseen.
  - When you use Arcane Cascade, you gain one of them as an aftereffect until the end of your next turn. It ends early if the stance ends, or under normal rules (e.g., a hostile action ends invisibility).
  - At 18+, false vitality and resist energy are heightened to 4th rank. You can take the feat again for the remaining spells.
  
  — [AoN Arcane Shroud](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?ID=9083)
- Legacy forum observation: the magus is complex and has fewer "effective" actions than simple martials, so the best choice changes round to round. Recharging via focus spells is an option, not an obligation. — [Paizo forums](https://paizo.com/threads/rzs2lqoc)
- Know Direction notes that some legacy magi felt pushed to "min max and focus on cantrips". The remaster's extra slots are meant to give flexibility for utility spells. — [Know Direction](https://knowdirectionpodcast.com/2026/07/investing-in-impossible-magic/)

### Inferences
- **Arcane Shroud is level 14 in the remaster,** not 16, and competes with Hasted Assault at 14. Choosing invisibility means entering Cascade makes Cyrus invisible, so his first Strike is likely against an off-guard target (Laughing Shadow bonus). The stance lockout and "until end of next turn" limit this to roughly once per two rounds.
- **Fleet Step stacking.** Fleet Step's status Speed bonus doesn't stack with Laughing Shadow's status bonus; take the higher. It does enlarge Dimensional Assault's range.
- **Checklist for a typical Cyrus turn:**
  1. Is Spellstrike charged? If not, plan Dimensional Assault (if a focus point is available and you need to move) or Running Recharge as the recharge.
  2. Is the target off-guard (flanked with the Champion, prone, invisible approach)? If not, can one action make it so: Trip, Improvise Strategy, or a Stride to flank?
  3. Spend MAP 0 on the Spellstrike or the setup roll, never on a throwaway Strike.
  4. Use the 3rd action for recharge or movement, Bon Mot, Raise Shield (if any), or Stride out of reach, not a Strike at −10.
- **Cast directly instead of Spellstriking** when:
  - an area spell would hit several enemies and you lack Expansive or the geometry is bad;
  - you're out of melee reach and moving would waste the turn;
  - the spell is a buff or utility spell;
  - the target's AC is extreme and you'd risk a critical-failure Strike with an attack-roll spell. Save spells are safer, because only a critical failure ruins them.
