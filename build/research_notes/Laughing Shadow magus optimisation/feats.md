# Feat choices for a remastered human Laughing Shadow Magus (Cyrus): reach-weapon trips, Lore and court play

> **Most important finding:** The Magus has been **remastered in *Impossible Magic*** (2026). Archives of Nethys (AoN) now shows the *Impossible Magic* Magus as the current version, with a separate Legacy (Secrets of Magic) page. Much of the user's current plan (feat levels, Sprinting Spellstrike, Laughing Shadow mechanics) looks like it was written from the **legacy** class. Every class-feat level in the plan has to be checked against *Impossible Magic*. **Laughing Shadow's damage now requires a free hand AND an off-guard target. That fits a trip build very well, because prone targets are off-guard.**
>

## 1. Remastered Magus class feats: levels, sources and community ratings

### Takeaway
The Magus is now an *Impossible Magic* class. Confirmed remastered feats so far are **Distracting Spellstrike (4, Laughing Shadow)** and **Sprinting Spellstrike (12, now needs a line-area spell, no hybrid-study prerequisite)**. Laughing Shadow's bonus damage now depends on a free hand plus an off-guard target. Early reviews call the remaster a big upgrade (more spell slots, better action economy). I could not get per-feat community ratings for the remastered list.

### Cited Findings
- AoN's current Magus page gives its source as ***Impossible Magic*, pg. 9** and links to a separate Legacy version, so the *Impossible Magic* Magus is the live (remaster) version. — [AoN Magus class](https://2e.aonprd.com/Classes.aspx?ID=74)
- Feats named on the remastered class page: Burning Cascade, Devastating Spellstrike, Distracting Spellstrike. — [AoN Magus class](https://2e.aonprd.com/Classes.aspx?ID=74)
- **Laughing Shadow (remaster)**, *Impossible Magic* pg. 13, PFS Standard, has a Legacy version (Hybrid Studies ID=2). While in Arcane Cascade:
  - +5-ft status bonus to Speeds, or +10 ft if unarmored.
  - When you Strike an **off-guard** creature **with a free hand**, Arcane Cascade's extra damage rises to **3 (5 with weapon specialization, 7 with greater weapon specialization)**.
  - The other hand must be *completely* free. Holding a free-hand weapon or any item in it disables the extra damage.
  - Conflux spell: **Dimensional Assault**. Studious spells: **Blur (7th), Ghostly Weapon (11th), Translocate (13th)**.
  - — [AoN Laughing Shadow](https://2e.aonprd.com/HybridStudies.aspx?ID=11)
- **Distracting Spellstrike**, Feat 4, two actions, *Impossible Magic* pg. 19.
  - Prerequisites: Laughing Shadow, Spellstrike. Requirements: a free hand and a charged Spellstrike.
  - Effect: Spellstrike plus a Feint against the target, with the Feint resolved just before the Strike. In Arcane Cascade, the Deception check gets a status bonus equal to your Arcane Cascade damage (1/2/3).
  - Remaster version; a Legacy version exists.
  - — [AoN Distracting Spellstrike](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?ID=9053)
- **Sprinting Spellstrike**, Feat 12, two actions, *Impossible Magic* pg. 24, PFS Standard.
  - Prerequisite: Spellstrike only. **No Laughing Shadow prerequisite is listed.** Requirement: a charged Spellstrike.
  - Effect: Spellstrike **with a spell that has a line area**. Before the Strike, Stride in a straight line up to the line's length, through enemies, without triggering reactions. You can use a Burrow, Climb, Fly or Swim Speed instead.
  - Special: with Expansive Spellstrike, the line follows your movement path.
  - — [AoN Sprinting Spellstrike](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?ID=9082)
- RPGBOT podcast review of *Impossible Magic*:
  - The magus gets "more spell slots" and "a better action economy". Spellstrike "finally feels like the centerpiece of the class", where before it was "an elaborate three-action hostage situation".
  - It names **Running Recharge, Sprinting Spellstrike, Twofold Twine and Volatile Spark** as feats that make the class "more mobile, flexible, and gloriously anime".
  - It calls the class "one of the biggest glow-ups" in the book.
  - The excerpt I could read gave no mechanics or per-feat ratings.
  - — [RPGBOT podcast S6E104](https://rpgbot.net/pf2e-impossible-magic-review-pt-1-finally-the-summoner-reads-its-own-job-description-rpgbot-podcast-s6e104/)
- Other sources confirming the book exists and its 2026 timing: [Bell of Lost Souls (Apr 2026): "Pathfinder unleashes four magical new classes in the upcoming Impossible Magic"](https://www.belloflostsouls.net/2026/04/rpg-pathfinder-unleashes-four-magical-new-classes-in-the-upcoming-impossible-magic.html); [Know Direction: Investing In Impossible Magic at PaizoCon 2026](https://knowdirectionpodcast.com/2026/06/investing-in-impossible-magic-at-paizocon-2026/); Paizo threads such as ["Impossible Magic magus / summoner"](https://paizo.com/threads/rzs8i3ib) and ["Behind The Times: Magus & Summoner"](https://paizo.com/threads/rzs8udmt). The Paizo threads could not be fetched.

### Inferences
- **Trip synergy (strong).** A successful Trip leaves the target prone, and prone creatures are off-guard (core rule, prior knowledge). So a Strength trip build with a **one-handed reach trip weapon** keeps a hand free and turns its own trips into the remastered Laughing Shadow damage bonus (3/5/7).
  - The free hand must hold nothing, which rules out a shield, staff or second weapon in that hand.
  - Two-handed reach weapons (guisarme, spiked chain and similar) switch off the damage bonus. **This affects weapon choice and Fused Staff** (see below).
- **Distracting Spellstrike (4) vs Expansive Spellstrike (4).**
  - Distracting applies off-guard through a Deception Feint. Cyrus is Strength/Int and probably low Charisma, so it fits him less well than tripping. It is still a way to switch on Laughing Shadow damage when a Trip fails or the target can't be tripped.
  - Expansive Spellstrike is still in the user's plan at 4. I could not confirm its remaster text or level.
- **Sprinting Spellstrike (12) is now narrower.** It needs line-area spells, and the arcane list has few of them (e.g. lightning bolt, prior knowledge). It gains a lot from Expansive Spellstrike. Before keeping it at 12, the user should check which line spells Cyrus will have prepared.
- **Fused Staff vs Running Recharge at 8.**
  - Fused Staff (legacy, prior knowledge) merges a staff into the weapon so it doesn't use up a hand. That matters more now, because Laughing Shadow needs a *completely* free hand and a held staff would break it. Its remaster text and level still need checking.
  - Running Recharge is confirmed to exist in the remaster ([RPGBOT podcast](https://rpgbot.net/pf2e-impossible-magic-review-pt-1-finally-the-summoner-reads-its-own-job-description-rpgbot-podcast-s6e104/)), but its level and text were not retrieved. From the name, it probably combines movement with recharging Spellstrike, which suits a mobile Laughing Shadow.
- **Reactive Strike at 6.** Reactive Strike is the remaster name for Attack of Opportunity (prior knowledge). A reach weapon makes it much better, since the threatened area is larger. I did not confirm whether the remastered magus list includes it at 6.

### Gaps
- Not verified for the *Impossible Magic* Magus: level, prerequisites and text of **Magus's Analysis, Force Fang, Expansive Spellstrike, Reactive Strike, Fused Staff, Running Recharge, Spell Swipe, Dimensional Disappearance, Hasted Assault, Arcane Shroud, Dispelling Spellstrike, Capture Magic, Knowledge Is Power, Spell Retaliation, Twofold Twine, Volatile Spark, Burning Cascade, Devastating Spellstrike**. Some may have been removed, renamed or moved. - Laughing Shadow's remaster studious spells no longer include Dimensional Disappearance-type options; the conflux spell is Dimensional Assault. I could not confirm whether **Dimensional Disappearance (10)** survives or still requires Laughing Shadow.
- No community ratings of the remastered magus feats were found: Reddit, the RPGBOT class guide (not on the allowlist) and the Paizo threads were all unreachable. Legacy-era ratings (Expansive Spellstrike and Laughing Shadow as staples, for example) may not carry over.

## 2. Improvise Strategy and Battle Planner (Warfare Lore feats)

### Takeaway
Neither feat comes from *Battlecry!*.
- **Improvise Strategy** is an *uncommon* level-7 skill feat from **Pathfinder #223: Hell's Destiny**. It requires **Master in Warfare Lore** and can make a foe **off-guard** with an Int-based Warfare Lore check, which directly turns on Laughing Shadow's bonus damage.
- **Battle Planner** is a common level-2 skill feat from **Player Core 2** (remastered). It requires **Expert in Warfare Lore** and lets a pre-rolled Warfare Lore check replace initiative.

### Cited Findings
- **Improvise Strategy**, one action, **Feat 7**.
  - Traits: Uncommon, Bravado, Concentrate, General, Mental, Skill.
  - Source: ***Pathfinder #223: Hell's Destiny* pg. 231**. Prerequisite: **Master in Warfare Lore**. PFS Limited.
  - You add the auditory or visual trait and roll Warfare Lore against the Perception DC of a creature within 30 ft.
  - **Critical success:** the target gets clumsy 1, dazzled, **off-guard**, or stupefied 1 (your choice) until the end of your next turn, with no immunity.
  - **Success:** the same effect, but the target becomes immune for 1 hour.
  - **Critical failure:** you are off-guard until the start of your next turn.
  - The target can end the effect early with a concentrate action (auditory) or an Interact to rub its eyes (visual).
  - Special: troop-leading use once per day.
  - — [AoN Improvise Strategy](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?ID=8982)
- **Battle Planner**, **Feat 2**, General/Skill, ***Player Core 2* pg. 227**, **Remastered** (a Legacy version exists). Prerequisite: **Expert in Warfare Lore**.
  - After scouting, or getting a detailed report, with clear knowledge of the foes' number, positions and identities, you spend 1 minute and roll Warfare Lore.
  - If the information is still accurate when initiative is rolled, you can use that result as your initiative. This is a fortune effect.
  - — [AoN Battle Planner](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?ID=6464); also [ID=2112](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?ID=2112)
- Related Warfare Lore feats from the same adventure (*Hell's Destiny*):
  - **Tactical Acumen** (Feat 2, uncommon, Expert Warfare Lore): use Warfare Lore to Recall Knowledge on troops. — [AoN Tactical Acumen](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?ID=8984)
  - **As on the Board, so on the Battlefield** (Feat 7, uncommon, Master Games Lore): Games Lore stands in for Warfare Lore. — [AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?ID=8981)

### Inferences
- **Improvise Strategy works very well with remastered Laughing Shadow.** For one action and an Int-keyed Lore check, the target becomes off-guard until the end of your next turn. That enables the free-hand bonus damage (3/5/7) without a Trip check, which helps against targets that are hard or impossible to trip, such as flying, legless or huge creatures. It also helps allies (−2 AC), and dazzled or stupefied are useful alternatives.
  - The plain success result grants 1-hour immunity, so in practice it is **one reliable use per enemy per fight**, with repeat uses only on a critical success.
- **Main costs:**
  - It is **uncommon**, so it needs GM access. It comes from the Hellbreakers adventure, so access is campaign-dependent.
  - It is **PFS Limited**.
  - It needs **Master Warfare Lore by 7**. For a magus that means skill increases at 3 and 7 go into Warfare Lore, competing with Athletics (trip accuracy) and Arcana. Additional Lore improves its Lore automatically at 3, 7 and 15 (prior knowledge, unverified here). If that holds, Warfare Lore reaches master at 7 for free, which makes this feat very attractive for Cyrus. **Verify the remaster text of Additional Lore.**
- **Battle Planner** is a reasonable 2nd-level pick with Expert Warfare Lore (from Additional Lore at 3). It is situational: it only applies when you have scouted, so it does nothing in ambushes. It overlaps with Incredible Initiative, though fortune and circumstance effects can stack in different ways.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm whether *Battlecry!* (2025) adds other Warfare Lore skill feats. The search results only pointed to [Know Direction's "Investing in Battlecry"](https://knowdirectionpodcast.com/2025/07/investing-in-battlecry/), which I did not fetch.
- No community assessment of Improvise Strategy was found. The feat is recent (AP #223).

## 3. Human ancestry feats and Axuma's Awakening

### Takeaway
**Axuma's Awakening is not an ancestry feat.**
- It is a **Rare level-11 general feat** from ***Pathfinder #191: The Destiny War*** (Stolen Fate AP), marked **Legacy**.
- It requires **exposure to the Well of Axuma**, so it is GM- and campaign-gated.
- It grants two at-will arcane cantrips plus a 1st- and a 2nd-rank arcane innate spell, each once per day.

I could not verify human ancestry feat levels in this session.

### Cited Findings
- **Axuma's Awakening**: Feat 11, Rare, General.
  - Source: *Pathfinder #191: The Destiny War* pg. 71. Prerequisite: "Exposure to the Well of Axuma". AoN marks it **Legacy Content**.
  - Effect: choose two arcane cantrips and cast them at will as arcane innate spells, heightened to half your level rounded up. Choose one 1st-level and one 2nd-level arcane spell, each castable once per day as an innate spell. All must be common or spells you have access to.
  - — [AoN Axuma's Awakening](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?ID=4161)

### Inferences
- For Cyrus, Axuma's Awakening would use a **general** feat slot at 11, which the plan currently fills with skill or general feats such as Incredible Initiative. It is an ancestry slot only if the GM swaps it in.
- Value is moderate. A magus already has arcane cantrips. Innate 1st/2nd-rank spells cast once per day are minor at level 11, apart from utility (e.g. invisibility, see the unseen) or extra cantrips for Spellstrike. It is likely to be taken mainly for story reasons if the campaign involves the Well of Axuma.
- Human feats, from prior knowledge (unverified this session; remaster *Player Core* versions):
  - **Natural Ambition (1)**: an extra 1st-level class feat. Standard for classes with strong 1st-level feats.
  - **General Training (1)**: a 1st-level general feat, e.g. Fleet or Canny Acumen.
  - **Clever Improviser (5)**: untrained skill checks with Untrained Improvisation. Low value for an Int character who already has many trained skills.
  - **Adaptive Adept**: an extra cantrip or 1st-rank spell. Thematic for a magus.
  - **Advanced General Training**.
  - **Multitalented (9)**: a free 2nd-level multiclass dedication.
  - **Unconventional Expertise**: needs Unconventional Weaponry, which raises proficiency in the chosen weapon. Possibly relevant if the chosen reach weapon is advanced or uncommon.
  - Clever Improviser is often called weak for Int-heavy builds because they are already trained in most skills.

In particular, I did not confirm whether Multitalented still waives attribute prerequisites in the remaster, or whether that waiver now belongs to Aiuvarin.

## 4. Best Multitalented dedication at 9

### Takeaway
I found no sources that evaluate dedications for the remastered magus. The reasoning below is mine, clearly marked. **Commander (Int key, Warfare Lore theme)**, **Witch/Psychic (Int casting focus)** and **Rogue (skills)** fit Cyrus best. Champion's armor benefits conflict with Laughing Shadow's unarmored speed bonus.

### Cited Findings
- Laughing Shadow's speed bonus is +5 ft, or **+10 ft if unarmored**, and the damage bonus needs a completely free hand. These are the main constraints on dedication choice. — [AoN Laughing Shadow](https://2e.aonprd.com/HybridStudies.aspx?ID=11)

### Inferences
These are based on prior knowledge and were not verified this session.
- **Commander (*Battlecry!*)**: Int-keyed, with tactics and a strong Warfare Lore / court-strategist theme. It probably gives support tactics without competing for hands. Check whether its dedication needs a 14 Int (Cyrus meets that) and whether its tactics need the commander's banner held in a hand. A held banner would conflict with the free hand.
- **Witch (Int)**: a familiar (utility, extra skill help), Int-based hexes and cantrips. Reasonable flavor for a scholar.
- **Psychic**: an Int or Cha key. Amps on cantrips and good flavor, but the remastered psychic's dedication value is debated (see the [Paizo thread "Un-Remaster Psychic"](https://paizo.com/threads/rzs8iva4), title only).
- **Rogue**: extra skill feats and training (social or court play), and possibly the Rogue's sneak-attack line. Sneak Attack and Laughing Shadow both pay off on off-guard targets, but the dedication's Sneak Attacker feat costs extra feats. The 14 Dex prerequisite matters only if Multitalented doesn't waive it.
- **Exemplar (*War of Immortals*)**: Str-friendly, but ikon-based weapon or body tricks may compete with Spellstrike for actions.
- **Champion**: armor and reactions. Wearing armor loses the +10 ft unarmored bonus. Weak fit.
- **Fighter**: mostly redundant, since a magus already has martial proficiency.
- **Sorcerer or Bard**: extra spells, but both are Cha-keyed, which is poor for Cyrus.
- **Marshal**: auras that need Str or Cha. Viable for a Str build, but Commander probably replaces it thematically.

### Gaps
- No community or RPGBOT rankings were found for Multitalented dedications on the remastered magus.
- Commander dedication prerequisites and hand requirements were not verified.

## 5. Skill and general feats for an Athletics trip build with Lore and court play

### Takeaway
I could not fetch AoN pages for most skill feats in this session. The main verified items are the Warfare Lore feats (Section 2). Below are prior-knowledge assessments, which need AoN confirmation.

### Cited Findings
- Battle Planner (PC2, 2, Expert Warfare Lore) and Improvise Strategy (Hell's Destiny, 7, Master Warfare Lore, uncommon) are confirmed; see Section 2 for details and sources. — [AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?ID=6464); [AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?ID=8982)

### Inferences
All from prior knowledge and not verified this session.
- **Athletics trip core:**
  - **Titan Wrestler**: Trip or Grapple larger creatures. Valuable for a trip build, since big monsters are common.
  - **Assurance (Athletics)**: mostly for climbing and swimming. Weak for combat trips, because the fixed result rarely beats a monster's Reflex DC at level.
  - **Combat Climber**: niche.
  - **Quick Jump and Powerful Leap**: mobility, which overlaps with Laughing Shadow's existing speed and teleport options.
  - **Kip Up**: an Acrobatics feat, so it doesn't fit an Athletics-only build.
- **Lore and Int skills:**
  - **Unmistakable Lore** (Lore, prevents critical failures on Recall Knowledge): reliable for a Lore-identity character.
  - **Magical Shorthand**: cheaper learning of spells, which suits a prepared caster.
  - **Unified Theory** (legendary Arcana, level 15): use Arcana for all magic traditions' checks.
  - **Cognitive Crossover**: a reroll-like benefit on Recall Knowledge. Source and level unverified.
  - **Trick Magic Item**: low value for an arcane caster who already has arcane items.
- **Social and court:**
  - **Courtly Graces** (Society for nobility social interactions): good for Int-based court play without Charisma.
  - **Leverage Connections**: decent.
  - **Bon Mot**: uses Diplomacy, which is likely low for Cyrus. Weak fit.
- **General feats:**
  - **Fleet**: stacks with Laughing Shadow's speed bonus.
  - **Canny Acumen** and **Toughness**: defensive staples.
  - **Incredible Initiative**: strong.
  - **Prescient Planner / Prescient Consumable**: convenience picks.
