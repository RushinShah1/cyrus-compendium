## Q1. Best permanent items for a magus by level band (spellhearts, worn items, magus items, runes)

### Takeaway
Remastered spellhearts (Jolt Coil and Flaming Star, both level 3/8/12) have verified stats. Each one adds an elemental damage rider to the weapon it is affixed to, but the rider only applies after you cast that element's spell through the spellheart.

The Master Magus Ring (level 11, 1,250 gp, remastered) is the main magus-only item: once per day it gives a free conflux Focus Point and a free teleport. Community favourites are Propulsive Boots (rated "perfect for any Magus"), Spellstriker Staff, Staff of the Unblinking Eye, and the Shifting and Wounding runes.

Rune choice for Aspera depends mainly on whether the GM lets the relic carry property runes at all, since by default relics cannot.

### Cited Findings

**Spellheart rules**
- The spellheart trait comes from Secrets of Magic pg. 170 (legacy), per [AoN Spellheart trait](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=391):
  - You affix a spellheart with "Affix a Spellheart", which works like Affix a Talisman.
  - **An item can hold one spellheart or one talisman, not both.**
  - When casting a cantrip from a spellheart, you can use your own spell attack roll or spell DC if it is higher.
- **Jolt Coil** ([AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=4765)): Electricity, Magical, Spellheart; Treasure Vault (Remastered) pg. 126; affixed to armour or a weapon.
  - Base: L3, 60 gp. Armour gets electricity resistance 2; weapon gets +1d4. DC 17. Casts *electric arc* (no frequency listed).
  - Greater: L8, 475 gp. Resistance 3; +1d6; DC 24. Casts *lightning bolt* once per day.
  - Major: L12, 1,750 gp. Resistance 5; +1d8; DC 29. Casts 4th-rank *lightning bolt* once per day and *draw the lightning* once per day.
  - Weapon effect: "After you cast an electricity spell by activating the coil, your Strikes with the weapon deal extra electricity damage until the end of your next turn."
- **Flaming Star** ([AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=5153)): Fire, Magical, Spellheart; Impossible Magic pg. 235.
  - Base: L3, 55 gp. Spell attack +7 / DC 17. Armour fire resistance 2; weapon +1d4 fire after you cast a fire spell through the star. Casts *ignition*.
  - Greater: L8, 425 gp. +14 / DC 24. Resistance 5; +1d6. Casts *fireball* once per day.
  - Major: L12, 1,750 gp. +19 / DC 29. Resistance 10; +1d8. Casts 4th-rank *fireball* and *wall of fire*, each once per day.
- Paizo forum user "Spellheart Rating / Guide" made a ratings document. The thread notes that the guide originally didn't flag uncommon or legacy-only spellhearts (since added). The ratings themselves were not in the thread text. — [Paizo forums](https://paizo.com/threads/rzs72lc5)

**Magus-specific items**
- **Master Magus Ring** ([AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=4859)): Item 11, 1,250 gp; Arcane, Focused, Invested; Treasure Vault (Remastered) pg. 150; a legacy version also exists.
  - Passive: +2 item bonus to Arcana.
  - Free action (concentrate), once per day: gain 1 Focus Point usable only on a magus conflux spell. It is lost at the end of your turn.
  - Free action (concentrate), once per day: teleport to an unoccupied space you can see within your Speed. The activation fails if it would bring another creature.
  - Craft requirement: you must be a magus.
- **Ancestral Geometry** ([AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=4736)): Item 2, 30 gp; Uncommon, Invested, Magical, Tattoo; Treasure Vault (Remastered) pg. 119.
  - Reaction (concentrate, fortune), once per day, when you roll a saving throw of the noted type: replace the roll with "the d20 roll from your ancestor's visitation".
  - A legacy version also exists ([AoN legacy link](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=2202)).
- **Ring of Wizardry:** forum posters say this kind of item "was intentionally removed" in the remaster. Ravingdork pointed to the Endless Grimoire as the closest replacement: it is not tradition-locked but was "currently Legacy-only" when posted. The thread is undated; one poster speculated it might return in Impossible Magic. — [Paizo forums, "Ring of Wizardry in the Remaster"](https://paizo.com/threads/rzs7pqo7)

**Community ratings from Martial Magicks (remastered magus guide, 13 Sept 2026)** — [Martial Magicks](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/d/1E_MwD2ewUIRcb29tr0KXUBfCmXbBNe_2im4-5Q6fRmI/mobilebasic)
- **Worn items**
  - Propulsive Boots ★★★★: small passive Speed bonus; activating them makes you quickened for Striding. "a perfect item for any Magus!"
  - Charlatan's Cape [U] ★★★: extra Translocate once per day; its Deception bonus suits Laughing Shadows.
  - Greater Masquerade Scarf ★★★★
- **Held items**
  - Spacious Pouch ★★★★
  - Explorer's Yurt ★★★
  - Skeleton Key ★★★
  - Everlight Crystal ★★
- **Apex items**
  - Bracers of Strength ★★★★ (Strength-based magus)
  - Cloak of Swiftness ★★★★ (Dexterity-based magus)
  - Belt of Long Life ★★★
  - Crown of Intellect ★★: the guide says magus spell DC won't keep up with full casters.
- **Weapon runes**
  - Most property runes add +1d6 of an energy type, so the choice comes down to preference or campaign.
  - Shifting ★★★★: lets you swap traits or damage type to hit weaknesses and avoid resistances.
  - Wounding ★★★: persistent bleed on piercing or slashing weapons.
  - Grievous: magus doesn't naturally get critical specialization. The Bow, Spear and Sword versions (★★★) stand out "if you get them."
  - Runic Impression ★★★ (conflux spell): adds a property rune for 1 action and suppresses one if you are over the limit.
- **Armour runes**
  - Winged ★★★: flight once per hour at 25 ft.
  - Raiment ★★
- **Laughing Shadow (Arcane Cascade)** ★★★★
  - +5 ft status bonus to Speeds, or +10 ft if unarmoured.
  - Extra damage vs off-guard foes (3 / 5 / 7 at weapon specialization / greater weapon specialization) **only "with a one-handed weapon and the other hand completely empty"**.
- **Inexorable Iron** (the two-handed study) ★★★: notes that reach weapons cut down how many reactive strikes you provoke.
- **Spellstrike range:** spell reach equals your weapon's reach, so touch spells reach further when delivered with a reach weapon.

**Relic and rune interaction (critical for Aspera)**
- Relics can take fundamental runes normally. "The GM decides which property runes, if any, a relic can have"; by default they can't, "like any other specific item." — [AoN Relic advancement rules](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=1101)

### Inferences
- **Laughing Shadow vs a two-handed weapon:** a two-handed polearm cannot meet the one-handed-weapon, free-hand requirement for Laughing Shadow's off-guard bonus damage. Cyrus keeps only the Speed bonus and the study's other features. This makes items that add damage riders more valuable to him (spellhearts, property runes if allowed, Potency-type consumables). The GM should confirm this.
- **Spellheart slots:** one spellheart on Aspera (unless it holds a talisman) and one on armour.
  - A Jolt Coil on the weapon fits "cast the coil's cantrip through Spellstrike, then Strike again next turn with +1d4 electricity".
  - Whether Spellstrike can use a spell cast from an item, rather than from your own slots or cantrips, was not verified for the remaster (see Gaps).
- **Suggested shopping order** (inferred from the verified levels and community ratings):
  - L3–5: Jolt Coil or Flaming Star (55–60 gp); Ancestral Geometry (30 gp, uncommon); Retrieval Prisms (see Q3).
  - L7–8: Spellstriker Staff (360 gp, if fusing; see Q2); Greater spellheart (425–475 gp); Propulsive Boots.
  - L11: Master Magus Ring (1,250 gp). Its teleport also gives a Laughing Shadow mobility option that doesn't depend on the free hand.
  - L11–12: Greater Spellstriker Staff (1,400 gp); Major spellheart (1,750 gp).
  - L16: Major Spellstriker Staff (8,500 gp); an apex item (Bracers of Strength or Cloak of Swiftness, depending on key attribute) around L17+.
- **Shifting rune conflict:** Shifting is the community's top magus rune, but on a relic it is the GM's call. Spellstriker Staff has shifting built in, but the staff's property runes are suppressed while fused (Q2). So Shifting on Aspera is available only if the GM lets the relic take property runes.

### Gaps
- No verified remaster stats were obtained for: Clay Sphere, Phantasmal Doorknob, other spellhearts, Healer's Gloves, Lifting Belt, Armbands of Athleticism, Tactician's Helm, Boots of Bounding, Retrieval Belt, Endless Grimoire, Sunweave, the Mobile armour rune, and the Fearsome, Astral, Rooting, Frost, Shock, Flaming and Grievous runes. The searches and fetches needed (AoN item pages, RPGBOT) were blocked or not reached within budget.
- No "lesser magus items" (for example, a lower-level magus ring) were found; whether Impossible Magic added any is unverified.
- RPGBOT's magic item and magus valuations could not be fetched (URL not on the allowlist).
- Not verified for the remaster: whether Spellstrike can use a spell cast from a spellheart or staff.

## Q2. Staff vs wand vs scroll economics; good staves for magus; how Fused Staff works in the remaster

### Takeaway
Fused Staff is a level-8 magus feat in Impossible Magic. It merges a staff into your weapon during daily preparations (or as a 10-minute activity). The fused weapon gains the staff's activations and spells, the higher of the two items' fundamental runes applies, and **the staff's property runes are suppressed**.

The community's top staves are the Spellstriker Staff (L7 / L11 / L16, remastered) and the Staff of the Unblinking Eye. Wands are viewed as "spell slots that cost money", best for long-duration spells cast every day.

### Cited Findings
- **Fused Staff** (Feat 8; Arcane, Magus; *Impossible Magic* pg. 21) — [AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?ID=2864); a legacy version exists.
  - You fuse the staff into the weapon during daily preparations, or with a 10-minute manipulate activity.
  - The fusion lasts until you fuse again or the weapon is out of your possession for 24 hours.
  - The fused weapon gets the staff's activations and benefits, and can Cast a Spell if prepared.
  - Fundamental runes are shared; the higher potency and striking rune applies.
  - **The staff's property runes are suppressed.**
  - Only you can use the fused abilities.
- **Spellstriker Staff** (Magical, Staff; held in 1 hand; Treasure Vault (Remastered) pg. 134) — [AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=4792)
  - Base: L7, 360 gp. +1 striking shifting staff. Cantrip *ignition*; 1st *echoing weapon*, *sure strike*; 2nd *acid grip*, *telekinetic maneuver*.
  - Greater: L11, 1,400 gp. +2 striking shifting. Adds 3rd *echoing weapon*, *haste*; 4th *acid grip*, *weapon storm*.
  - Major: L16, 8,500 gp. +2 greater striking shifting. Adds 5th *blink charge*, *echoing weapon*, *vampiric feast*; 6th *acid grip*, *weapon storm*; 7th *blink charge*, *true target*.
  - Activation: free action, once per hour, when you Spellstrike with a non-cantrip spell and miss. Creatures in a 5-ft emanation take damage based on spell level (basic Reflex vs your spell DC; Will if the damage is mental).
  - Craft requirement: Spellstrike.
- **Martial Magicks staff ratings** — [Martial Magicks](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/d/1E_MwD2ewUIRcb29tr0KXUBfCmXbBNe_2im4-5Q6fRmI/mobilebasic)
  - Spellstriker's Staff ★★★★: "nearly everything a Magus wants". The guide notes Fused Staff was updated to account for it.
  - Staff of the Unblinking Eye [U] ★★★★: *detect magic*, scrying, *sure strike*. "Every Magus will want this staff if they can get it."
  - Staff of Protection ★★★ (*shield*, *resist energy*)
  - Staff of Phantasms ★★★
  - Staff of Arcane Might [R] ★★★
  - Staff of Fire ★★ (*ignition* is good, but its other spells depend on spell DC)
  - General advice: prefer staves whose spells rely less on your spell DC, because magus DC lags.
- **Wands and scrolls** (same guide) — [Martial Magicks](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/d/1E_MwD2ewUIRcb29tr0KXUBfCmXbBNe_2im4-5Q6fRmI/mobilebasic)
  - Wands "are spell slots that cost money", are expensive, and occupy a hand. Use them for long-duration daily spells.
  - Specialty wands are less desirable because they emulate spellshape feats or need a hand that hybrid studies often want free.
  - Spell sections recommend wands for all-day or long spells: *energy aegis*, *contingency*, *hidden mind*, *planar palace*, *imaginary lockbox*.
  - Scrolls are recommended for situational spells such as *banishment*, *veil of privacy* and *airlift*. The guide also notes that downtime crafting stretches resources.
- **Paizo forum thread "Fused Staff and Spellhearts":** exists, but its content was not fetched. — [Paizo forums](https://paizo.com/threads/rzs43gs2)

### Inferences
- **Fusing a staff into Aspera:** if the GM lets Aspera carry property runes, they stay active, but the fused staff's own property runes (for example, Spellstriker's shifting) are suppressed. Since Aspera already gets fundamental runes normally, the staff mainly adds spells, charges and the Spellstrike-miss explosion.
- **Custom staff:** a GM-approved custom staff should probably follow the Spellstriker Staff's pricing (360 / 1,400 / 8,500 gp at L7 / 11 / 16) as a benchmark.
- **Hands and Spellstrike:** Aspera is two-handed and Cyrus has no free hand, so a separate wand or staff costs Interact actions. This strengthens the case for Fused Staff over a held staff, and for scrolls only in out-of-combat use. Treat this as an inference; check the remaster's hand rules for Cast a Spell from a fused weapon.
- **Relic plus Fused Staff:** whether a relic can be the fusion target is a GM ruling. Relics are specific items whose property runes are GM-gated.

### Gaps
- Remaster rules on how staff charges are prepared for a magus (bounded caster) were not verified, including whether Fused Staff changes charge count.
- Staff of the Unblinking Eye's level, price and remaster status were not verified.
- No numeric community comparison (gp per spell) of staff vs wand vs scroll was found.

## Q3. Consumables for a two-handed magus

### Takeaway
The Retrieval Prism is now a remastered (Impossible Magic) L3, 12 gp consumable talisman affixed to **armour**. It is a free action that needs a free hand, and its Greater version (L10, 200 gp) needs no attunement. The remastered magus guide rates the Potion of Quickness ★★★★ ("Another source of haste!") and lists Cat's Eye Elixir and Mistform Elixir as favourites.

Many items on the list could not be verified. Prices from an older forum thread are legacy and may differ in the remaster.

### Cited Findings
- **Retrieval Prism** (Consumable, Magical, Talisman, Teleportation; *Impossible Magic* pg. 225) — [AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=5108)
  - Base: L3, 12 gp. Affixed to armour; free action (concentrate); requires a free hand.
  - When you affix it, you attune it to one item of 1 Bulk or less; activating it teleports that item into your hand.
  - It can't retrieve items off-plane or inside extradimensional spaces. An unexpended prism can be re-attuned.
  - Greater: L10, 200 gp. No attunement; retrieves any item in your possession of 1 Bulk or less.
  - A Paizo thread asked whether its range is unlimited (content not fetched). — [Paizo forums](https://paizo.com/threads/rzs43ukj)
- **Martial Magicks consumable ratings** — [Martial Magicks](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/d/1E_MwD2ewUIRcb29tr0KXUBfCmXbBNe_2im4-5Q6fRmI/mobilebasic)
  - Potion of Quickness ★★★★
  - Greater Potion of Flying ★★★★ (lasts 1 hour)
  - Potion of Flying ★★★
  - Potion of Leaping ★★★
  - Unrated favourites: Healing Potions, Cat's Eye Elixir (concealed or hidden foes), Darkvision Elixir, Mistform Elixir (concealment), Elixir of Life.
- **Legacy-era prices** from an undated, pre-APG Paizo thread; treat as legacy, since remaster prices may differ — [Paizo forums](https://paizo.com/threads/rzs432zs)
  - Cat's Eye Elixir: L2, 7 gp.
  - Mistform Elixir: lesser L4, 18 gp; moderate L6, 56 gp.
  - Healing Potion: minor L1, 4 gp; lesser L3, 12 gp; moderate L6, 50 gp.
  - Emerald Grasshopper (talisman): L5, 30 gp; "greatly improves High Jump".
  - Oil of Potency: L2, 7 gp; temporary +1 striking weapon.
  - Potion of Flying: L8, 100 gp.
  - Swift Block Cabochon: L7, 70 gp.

### Inferences
- Retrieval Prism's free-hand requirement makes it awkward for a two-handed wielder mid-combat. It is better for pulling a scroll or potion after releasing one hand (a free action), or for re-grabbing Aspera after being disarmed (Aspera must be 1 Bulk or less). It now affixes to armour, so it competes with an armour spellheart for that item's single talisman/spellheart slot.
- Potion of Quickness (quickened, often usable for Strike or Stride) matches the guide's emphasis on haste effects. It pairs with Time-relic gifts such as Borrow Time and Temporal Pulse (Q4).

### Gaps

  - Potion Patch, Numbing Tonic, Soothing Tonic, Transmuting Ingot, Potency Crystal
  - Ghostbane Fulu, Escape Fulu, Blast Boots, Energizing Tea, "missives"
  - Potion of Quickness (level and price)
  - Remaster prices of Healing Potions and elixirs
  - Fetch budget ran out and RPGBOT was blocked. These need checking on AoN.

## Q4. Relic rules: gift tiers and levels, aspects (Time, Mind), named gifts

### Takeaway
A relic's level equals its bearer's level. It typically gains one gift per 4 levels:
- Minor gifts at 1st and 5th
- Major gifts at 9th and 13th
- A grand gift at 17th

The GM chooses gifts and decides whether property runes are allowed. **Correction to the brief:** the Time aspect and its gifts are sourced on AoN to *Treasure Vault (Remastered)* pg. 200, not GM Core. The Mind aspect gifts (including Repository of Knowledge) are in GM Core pg. 316.

### Cited Findings
**Advancement rules** — [AoN Relic advancement rules](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=1101)
- A relic's level always equals its bearer's.
- Fundamental runes are allowed. Property runes are at the GM's discretion and not allowed by default.
- Gifts arrive roughly one per 4 bearer levels, and pacing can follow the story. The page's example: 4th, 7th, 13th, 16th.
- Table 2–22, relic gifts:

| Gift # | Minimum level | Type | Gold-piece equivalent |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1st | Minor | 20 gp |
| 2 | 5th | Minor | 160 gp |
| 3 | 9th | Major | 700 gp |
| 4 | 13th | Major | 3,000 gp |
| 5 | 17th | Grand | 15,000 gp |

- Guidance:
  - The GM picks the gift, or offers two paths to choose from.
  - Gifts should "complement the bearer" and keep a cohesive theme.
  - Avoid minor gifts at 10th level or higher.
  - The gold-piece equivalent is used to reduce other treasure.
- AoN didn't say whether this rules page is GM Core or legacy Gamemastery Guide text; a legacy-flagged version was also linked ([AoN ID 1096](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=1096)).

**Aspects listed on AoN** — [AoN Relics](https://2e.aonprd.com/Relics.aspx)
- Air, Artistry, Beast, Celestial, Death, Dragon, Earth, Emotion, Fiend, Fire, Forge, Intelligent Relic, Life, Luck, Metal, Mind, Plant, Shadow, Soul, Time, Water.

**Time gifts** (Treasure Vault (Remastered) pg. 200; paraphrased) — [AoN Time aspect](https://2e.aonprd.com/Relics.aspx?Aspect=18)
- **Borrow Time** (Minor)
  - 2 actions (concentrate, manipulate); range 30 ft.
  - The target is quickened for 1 round, then slowed 1 for 1 round, then immune until your next daily preparations.
- **Sands of the Hourglass** (Minor; relic level 5+)
  - 2 actions; once per hour; 30 ft; Fortitude save.
  - Success: clumsy 1 + enfeebled 1.
  - Failure: clumsy 2, enfeebled 2, drained 1.
  - Critical failure: clumsy 3, enfeebled 3, drained 2.
  - Clumsy and enfeebled last 1 minute.
- **Precognition** (Major; prediction)
  - Reaction, once per day, at the start of your turn.
  - Resolve two of your turns, then choose which one happens.
- **Temporal Pulse** (Major)
  - 2 actions; once per day; 30 ft; Will save.
  - Failure: slowed 1. Critical failure: slowed 2.
  - For each creature slowed, one ally within 30 ft (including you) becomes quickened for 1 round.
- **Time Jump** (Major)
  - 1 action; once per day; the relic casts *time jump* on you.
- **Time Reversal** (Grand)
  - 1 action; once per day; the relic casts *time beacon* on you.

**Mind gifts** (GM Core pg. 316 unless noted) — [AoN Mind aspect](https://2e.aonprd.com/Relics.aspx?Aspect=9)
- **Linguistic Nexus** (Minor): one extra language per 2 relic levels.
- **Recalculate** (Minor): reaction after you miss an attack; +1 circumstance bonus to your next attack against that target before your next turn.
- **Repository of Knowledge** (Minor): trained in 3 GM-chosen Lore skills; expert at relic level 9, master at 17.
- **Perception Filter** (Major): 2 actions, once per day. For 10 minutes, creatures entering a 120-ft emanation make a secret Will save. Results range up to a −2 Perception penalty or treating you as hidden, lasting 1 day.
- **Psychic Scream** (Major): 2 actions; 20-ft burst; basic Will save; 1d10 mental damage per 2 relic levels. On a critical failure, stupefied 1 for 1 minute.
- **Mental Bastion** (Grand): telepathy; reduces stupefied by 1; permanently quickened (the extra action can only Recall Knowledge).
- **Ancestors' Call** (Minor; dwarf-themed; Pathfinder #193 pg. 85).
- Example of another aspect for comparison: **Plant** (GM Core pp. 316–317) has scaling damage minors (Pollen Spray: 1d6 per 2 levels, 15-ft cone) and once-per-day majors. — [AoN Plant aspect](https://2e.aonprd.com/Relics.aspx?Aspect=10)

### Inferences
- **Suggested gift path for Aspera, levels 3–16** (four gifts; the grand gift at 17 is outside the range):
  1. **Minor 1 (L1–4):** Repository of Knowledge (could cover Warfare Lore plus 2 others, making expert by 9), or Recalculate. Recalculate fits a reach magus whose Spellstrike sometimes misses, though it doesn't recover the spell.
  2. **Minor 2 (L5+):** Sands of the Hourglass. It's repeatable once per hour and clumsy lowers AC/Reflex, which supports Trip follow-ups and allies.
  3. **Major 1 (L9):** Temporal Pulse (party-wide action economy), or Precognition (once-per-day turn insurance).
  4. **Major 2 (L13):** whichever of the two above wasn't taken, or Time Jump for mobility (fits Laughing Shadow).
- **Wealth offset:** the gift gold-piece equivalents (20 / 160 / 700 / 3,000 gp) mean the GM may cut Cyrus's treasure by roughly those amounts. This is small compared with party treasure (Q5).
- **Mixing aspects:** combining Time (Treasure Vault (Remastered)) and Mind (GM Core) is standard practice, since relics normally have two aspects. Confirm with the GM.

### Gaps
- Not confirmed: whether GM Core itself contains the advancement table above, or only the legacy Gamemastery Guide. AoN labelling was ambiguous.
- Relic seed rules (choosing two aspects, base relic level) were not fetched.
- No community reports (Reddit or forums) on how other groups use relics were found within budget.

## Q5. Wealth-by-level expectations (GM Core)

### Takeaway
GM Core Table 10-9 gives party treasure for 4 PCs per level. Each level a party gets two permanent items each of level+1 and level, consumables, and currency. Roughly a quarter of the value is one PC's share.

### Cited Findings
- Source: GM Core pg. 59, Table 10-9, Party Treasure by Level (4 PCs). Each level also includes 2 permanent items of level+1 and 2 of the current level, and consumables at level+1, level and level−1. — [AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2656)

| Level | Total value | Party currency | Currency per extra PC |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | 500 gp | 120 gp | 30 gp |
| 4 | 850 gp | 200 gp | 50 gp |
| 5 | 1,350 gp | 320 gp | 80 gp |
| 6 | 2,000 gp | 500 gp | 125 gp |
| 7 | 2,900 gp | 720 gp | 180 gp |
| 8 | 4,000 gp | 1,000 gp | 250 gp |
| 9 | 5,700 gp | 1,400 gp | 350 gp |
| 10 | 8,000 gp | 2,000 gp | 500 gp |
| 11 | 11,500 gp | 2,800 gp | 700 gp |
| 12 | 16,500 gp | 4,000 gp | 1,000 gp |
| 13 | 25,000 gp | 6,000 gp | 1,500 gp |
| 14 | 36,500 gp | 9,000 gp | 2,250 gp |
| 15 | 54,500 gp | 13,000 gp | 3,250 gp |
| 16 | 82,500 gp | 20,000 gp | 5,000 gp |

- At 1st level, many permanent items should be mundane gear rather than magic items. — [AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2656)

### Inferences
- **Benchmarks against one PC's share of each level's treasure:**
  - At 11th, a 1,250 gp Master Magus Ring is about 11% of the party's 11,500 gp, close to half of one PC's roughly 2,875 gp share.
