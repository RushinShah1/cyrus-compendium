## Cascading Ray L 10 ['attack', 'magus'] action 1
Requirements Your last action was a successful Spellstrike, and the spell you cast dealt energy damage (acid, cold, electricity, fire, force, sonic, vitality, or void).Magical energy spills out to extend beyond the creature you struck. The flowing energy forms a ray with a range of 120 feet originating from the creature you damaged. You can't target any of the creatures you damaged with your Spellstrike with the ray. Make a spell attack against the new target's AC, at the same multiple attack penalty as the Strike. On a hit, the target of the ray takes 1d4 damage per spell rank of the spell you used for Spellstrike, or 1d8 damage per rank if you cast the spell from a spell slot. The ray uses the same damage type as the spell dealt (choose one if it dealt more than one type of damage). This ray has the attack trait, the tradition trait of the original spell, and the trait matching the damage type.

## Striker's Scroll L 4 ['magus'] passive None
You can attach a scroll to your weapon or @UUID[Compendium.pf2e.equipment-srd.Item.Handwraps of Mighty Blows] to blend its spell into an attack, carefully folding it around a part of the weapon or sealing it to the weapon's surface. Attaching a scroll requires using the @UUID[Compendium.pf2e.actionspf2e.Item.Affix a Talisman] action. You can have only one scroll affixed to a weapon at a time, and you can't have both a talisman and scroll attached.
You can Cast the Spell from the scroll as part of a Spellstrike, provided the spell is one you could normally use with Spellstrike. You can't Cast the Spell in any other way while it's affixed, though you can use Affix a Talisman again to remove the scroll and use it as a normal—if a bit crumpled—scroll.

You can flip open a book to simultaneously study and deflect attacks. When you Raise a Shield, you can raise a book you're wielding instead of a shield. While you have a book raised, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to AC and a +1 circumstance bonus to @UUID[Compendium.pf2e.actionspf2e.Item.Recall Knowledge] checks to identify creatures using a skill related to the subject of the book (Arcana if you're using your spellbook). If consulting the book normally grants an item bonus to the check for consulting the book, you gain this bonus as well.
If you have @UUID[Compendium.pf2e.feats-srd.Item.Shield Block], you can use a raised tome for that feat as though it were a shield. The tome has Hardness 3, HP 12, and BT 6.

The rune takes the form of a specialized diagram in your spellbook, occupying one page. The rune's benefits apply to any one tome in your possession chosen during your daily preparations. If your spellbook leaves your possession, the rune-granting benefit is suppressed until you get the book back.
